# Priority and provenance

The dated record of when each part of this framework was first written down, and how a
reader can check that date without taking the author's word for it.

## Changed since this record (added 15 November 2026; extended 21 November 2026)

The record in this folder is dated 31 July 2026 and is kept here as it was published. It is not the programme's present position on priority.

**A claim of priority has been withdrawn.** Until 12 November 2026 the programme claimed that no earlier document held the first four components together, and it published the condition that would end that claim: one earlier document holding them. Its comparison had cited Yudkowsky (2001), Creating Friendly AI, and graded it as holding less. A search of that work's whole text on that day found the four stated together. The claim of priority for the five components together is withdrawn.

The five components were set down on 8 December 2024, before any law of the theory was stated. Each has since been given a form that can be measured and refuted. The record of 8 December 2024 stands as the date of the author's own formulation. What the theory claims as its own is stated plainly: the equation, the five laws with their mathematics, and the propositions registered with what would refute each.

## What a date here does and does not establish

A date establishes when a statement existed. It does not establish that the statement is
correct, that it was first, or that no one else had reached it independently. Where earlier
or parallel work exists, it is named in the record and in the repository `README.md` rather
than left for a reader to find.
