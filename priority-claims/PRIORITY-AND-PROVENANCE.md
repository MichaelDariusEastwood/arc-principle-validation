## 4. What is claimed, and what is not

Priority is claimed only for what the anchored artefacts contain on their timestamped dates. The 8 December 2024 control-failure passage establishes the structural thesis ten days before Anthropic's alignment-faking result (Greenblatt et al., arXiv:2412.14093, 18 December 2024); no claim is made to their specific RL-training mechanism, and any citation of their 78 per cent figure carries both the RL-training condition and the 12 per cent baseline. Where independent researchers reached related results, credit runs to them wherever their dates require it: the register classes such rows concurrent or prior art explicitly (Sharma and Chopra on the quantified sequential result; Kitaev and Preskill on quantum error correction; o1 preceding the manuscript on deployed sequential reasoning). The convergence register stands at 26 dated events as at 31 July 2026, of which 22 are claimed as independent convergence and 4 are disclosed and not claimed. This document establishes when this author's artefacts existed; it does not assert that no one else has reasoned about recursion, correction, or scaling.
