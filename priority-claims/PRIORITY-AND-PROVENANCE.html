Do not read a file's OSF upload date, or its position in the version stack, as the date the idea was created. How this project's files are organised (so nothing is misread)

  • Canonical files at OSF Storage root carry no version number in the filename. Each document has exactly one canonical name; its full history is the Revisions tab on that file.
  • The version stack IS the history. Uploading a corrected file under the same name adds a new version on top — it never overwrites the earlier one. Example: Executive-Summary.pdf holds four versions, version 1 dated 17 March 2026, the latest dated 4 July 2026. Click Revisions on any file to see every dated version.
  • The archive/ folder holds superseded, separately-named earlier drafts (e.g. Eden-Vision-v2, Executive-Summary-v6) from the pre-canonical naming era. They are kept for the record and carry their own original March 2026 dates. They are historical copies, not the primary priority record — the primary record is the anchors in the table above and the Revisions tabs.
  • Nothing is ever deleted. Superseded material is moved to archive/, never removed.

What priority is, and is not, claimed

Priority is claimed only for what the anchored artifacts actually contain on their timestamped dates — the correction-requirement thesis and the U = I × R relation (8 Dec 2024), and the named Eden Protocol framing (30 Apr 2025). Where independent researchers reached related results, priority is credited to them in Paper XI wherever their dates require it; several convergences are explicitly logged as concurrent or later than the external work, not as claims against it. This document establishes when this author's own artifacts existed; it does not assert that no one else has reasoned about recursion, correction, or scaling.
