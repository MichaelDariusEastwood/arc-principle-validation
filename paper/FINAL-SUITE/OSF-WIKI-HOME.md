# The ARC Principle

## Overview

This project contains the complete document suite for the ARC Principle framework and the Eden Protocol alignment architecture, first proposed in *Infinite Architects: Intelligence, Recursion, and the Creation of Everything* (Eastwood, 2026).

**The Principle:**

U = I x R^α

Where:
- U = Effective capability
- I = Base potential (structured asymmetry)
- R = Recursive depth
- α = Scaling exponent (theoretical limit α ≤ 2)

**Key Derivation:** α = 1/(1−β), where β is self-referential coupling.

**Core Claim:** AI capability scales through recursive self-correction while external alignment constraints (RLHF, constitutional rules, output filters) do not participate in the recursive loop and therefore cannot compound. We define the *alignment scaling exponent* α_align as a measurable quantity and predict α_align ≈ 0 for external approaches versus α_align ≈ α_cap for architecturally embedded values. This prediction has not been tested. We provide the measurement protocol.

## Core Finding

**The form of recursion matters more than the amount of compute.**

Sequential processing (where each step builds on previous outputs) yields super-linear capability gains. Parallel processing (independent samples averaged together) yields diminishing returns. This structural observation underpins the alignment argument: if capability compounds recursively while safety constraints do not participate in the recursion, the safety ratio degrades with depth regardless of initial safety margins.
