## The issue
The scaling exponent α was measured super-linear early (Paper I α≈1.34; Paper II v11 α≈2.24) and later
**retracted to α≈0.49 (sub-linear) under six-model blinding** (Sharma & Chopra credited; Paper IV-d blinding,
Paper IX retraction). Two papers built on the original super-linear result have not fully reconciled with the
correction, in two different ways:

### Paper II — body corrected, TITLE lags
- **Title (h1):** "The ARC Principle: Experimental Validation of **Super-Linear** Error Suppression Through Sequential Recursive Processing"
- **Body verdict (verbatim):** "α_sequential > 1: **Not confirmed** cross-architecturally… yields α = 0.49, which is [sub-linear]"; "The claim that sequential recursion always yields α > 1 (super-linear), **is not supported**. The revised claim is that sequential recursion yields α > 0 (positive scaling)."
- So the body honestly downgraded α>1 → α>0, but the title still asserts VALIDATION of SUPER-LINEAR.

### Paper I — NO correction notice at all (worse)
- **Title:** "Preliminary Evidence for **Super-Linear** Capability Amplification Through Sequential Self-Reference"
- **Key finding (verbatim):** "Sequential recursion yields α ≈ 1.34 (**super-linear**)."
- Paper I carries **ZERO** correction reference (no retracted/superseded/blinding/0.49/does-not-replicate — verified by term sweep). The FOUNDATIONAL empirical paper presents the retracted super-linear claim as if current, breaking the corpus's own scrupulous correction-carrying standard (Paper II updated its body; HRIH carries the retraction 28×; Paper IX IS the retraction paper).
