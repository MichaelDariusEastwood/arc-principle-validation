## ONE genuine overstatement (confirmed)
**Paper III:** "[the metabolic derivations] guarantee that no physical process, no whale, no earthquake, no expanding universe, can achieve super-linear scaling. This is a mathematical certainty, not an empirical tendency."
- Overclaims in two ways: (1) universalises the α=d/(d+1) result — derived by WBE/Banavar/Demetrius for BIOLOGICAL/network-transport systems — to ALL physical processes (earthquakes follow Gutenberg-Richter; cosmological scaling has its own exponents); (2) elevates an empirically-grounded model to "a mathematical certainty" for all of physics.
- A physicist reviewer attacks this immediately. It is the corpus's most exposed physical-domain overclaim (F23-adjacent).

## Recommended temper (T5/operator — not unilateral; depends on Cauchy-framework scope)
Scope the claim to the model's domain, e.g.: "physical processes governed by finite-dimensional network transport — metabolism and systems in that class — cannot exceed α<1; within that model this is a mathematical consequence, not merely an empirical tendency." Retain the valid point (intelligence, in unbounded-dimension information space, breaks the geometric speed limit) without claiming the metabolic law as a universal certainty for earthquakes and cosmology.
- Why flag not fix: correct scoping depends on whether the Cauchy-unification framework (Paper VII) genuinely derives d/(d+1) for ALL finite-dimensional systems or only network-transport ones — a substantive scientific judgement for the author, not a wording tweak. Finalise with F23 recompute.
