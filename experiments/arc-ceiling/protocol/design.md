# Design recorded before pilot scoring

19 November 2026. This is a new development pilot, separate from Unit 82 (P17), Unit 83/83b (P21) and the Law IV retention and comparison propositions. Its purpose is to expose a measured difference, including a null or contrary difference. It does not amend a registered experiment.

**Question.** With fixed verifier votes and repair rights, how do final and undetected errors vary when we change verifier family, access to evidence and timing? Does externally orchestrated expert iteration on the resulting decisions erode or preserve installed task performance?

**Tasks.** Three finite-state, two-choice domains have exact judges. Compare a proposed arithmetic proof step, check a bounded operation in a static program specification, or enforce a simulated ledger quota. Every subsequent decision receives the preceding decision as part of its state. The public grammar is deliberately narrow. Held-out task tuples exclude training and anchor tuples; the same three rule templates occur on all splits. Every held-out trajectory is paired across treatments and nested across depths. The code domain uses inert bounds predicates. The ledger has no external actions.

**Step experiment.** Run both published pretrained transformer backbones in both generator/verifier directions, using a fitted binary output adapter. For each depth and vote count evaluate own-family terminal projection, own-family online, other-family online, other-family terminal projection and terminal full-trace controls. Include an exact checker as an instrument control. Only the most recent operand is intact in the terminal projection. Every treatment may edit every recorded action. Terminal repairs do not regenerate the original proposals. All model arms allocate K virtual binary votes per step, with shared random numbers for paired comparisons. Length of text, hardware and FLOPs differ. The finite forced-choice distribution is cached exactly, and repeated votes draw from that distribution. They are not separately generated free-form critiques.

**Endpoints.** Use the per-trajectory fraction of final actions disagreeing with an ideal replay, and the per-step incidence of an initially wrong proposal accepted without a flag against its local state. Report wrong repairs, confidence, Brier error and the proportion of locally wrong proposals. Estimate joint misses, marginal misses, excess over their product and the binary Pearson correlation on the same paired candidates. Separate natural from controlled, planted misses and report denominators. Report within-item dependence separately from mixture dependence. Sparse and constant outcomes may be unevaluable. Compare terminal full trace to the projection to examine information removal.

**Intervals and claims.** Report paired task-trajectory bootstrap intervals, conditional on this finite item bank and two checkpoints, with common resampling within domains. Use R=32, K=4 as the single descriptive endpoint and the other grid points as exploratory. Show all curves and both generator directions. An empirical warning boundary is a predeclared threshold on a measured error rate. An asymptotic capability ceiling, a universal critical depth, and actual alignment or harm cannot be estimated from the benchmark score alone. Model-reported probabilities concern task decisions and require a calibration check.

**Weight experiment.** The Pythia generator starts from a supervised capability-only fit, followed by an installed policy fit. Train a rank-one update to the two output-token rows and a two-token bias; keep transformer blocks fixed. In each of ten generations sample new on-policy action sequences, let the assigned critic revise their actions, and update the actor on the selected labels. Compare own terminal, own online, frozen cross-family online, own online with a ground-truth anchor and cross-family online with the same anchor. Hold seeds, generated examples and gradient-step counts fixed subject to feasible line searches. The anchor imposes a log-barrier on mean policy error in its own bank. The fresh rollout bank, anchor bank and evaluation bank are disjoint. Report held-out capability and policy performance separately, absolute changes, and retention only where the baseline installed gain exceeds two percentage points. A ten-generation constrained head update is actual parameter modification under external experimental control.

**Admission to a stronger claim.** For a later frontier or full-adapter study, measure capability on an independent calibrated scale across generator levels. Measure newly arriving burden, delivered repairs, error stock and resource exposure in commensurate units. Estimate any crossover on development strata and predict it on reserved model families before revealing their outcomes. A failed prediction, missing calibration, a crossover outside the range or a result driven entirely by the engineered projection is reported as such. No guarantee of a positive result, authorship approval, or academic deposit is implied.
