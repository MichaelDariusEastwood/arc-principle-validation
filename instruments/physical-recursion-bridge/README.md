## 1. Purpose

The ARC programme can investigate whether a common mathematical description of recursive composition is useful across AI, biological networks and selected physical systems. That is a legitimate scientific question. It is not yet established that one exponent or one mechanism governs all such domains, nor that recursion changes spacetime geometry, defines the arrow of time, or explains cosmogenesis.

This note provides a disciplined bridge between the existing programme and its physical-domain predictions. It preserves the cosmological question in HRIH / Recursive Creation while requiring each link in the chain to be stated as a separate hypothesis with its own measurement, rival explanation and falsification rule.

**Do not alter existing dated records to make them appear more prospective.** This note is written after the Grier time-crystal paper and its dataset became public. Reanalysis of that known dataset can compare models and improve the protocol; it cannot, by itself, be presented as independent prospective confirmation. A new experiment on data not yet observed, ideally run by a group independent of the author, is needed for strong confirmatory evidence.

## 3. Four meanings of “time” that must not be merged

1. **Relativistic time.** Proper time $\tau$ is the time accumulated along a physical worldline. Coordinate time and distant simultaneity depend on the frame and coordinate convention. Motion and gravitational potential affect clock comparisons; spacetime curvature is governed by the relativistic field equations. A change in a distant simultaneity convention does not transmit information or cause a distant event to change.
2. **Thermodynamic arrow.** Entropy production and irreversible processes help explain the macroscopic time-asymmetry seen in the world. Entropy is not the unit of time and is not a replacement for proper time or a clock. Landauer's bound concerns the thermodynamic cost of logically irreversible information erasure/reset; it does not assert that every computational step must dissipate the same minimum energy.
3. **Cycle time and dynamics.** An oscillator has a period and a count of cycles. A recurrent dynamical state can be periodic without being an algorithm that improves itself. A system counts as *self-correcting* for a specified target only if a perturbation protocol demonstrates recovery toward that target, with a measured error, recovery time and baseline comparison.
4. **Computational or experienced time.** For an AI experiment, record wall-clock time, hardware/energy use, inference steps, tokens and any actual weight/code updates separately. More iterations per second do not establish that a model subjectively experiences more time. Current benchmark results measure behaviour, not phenomenological experience.

The working bridge should therefore model at least distinct quantities: $N$ (physical cycles or algorithmic updates), $\tau$ (elapsed proper time where relevant), measured output $Q$, and energy/temperature/entropy variables where available. A correspondence between them is a hypothesis to test, not a definitional identity.

## 4. The conceptual gate: periodicity is not automatically recursion

The Grier, Morrell and Elliott acoustic system is a valuable candidate physical system because its nonreciprocal wave-mediated interactions sustain emergent oscillatory states. The reported system does **not** need intention to exhibit stable dynamics. That does not make every oscillation a recursive self-improvement process, and it does not by itself demonstrate self-correction.

Before applying ARC notation, state the proposed physical feedback map explicitly:

- **State vector:** bead positions/velocities, phase and amplitude variables chosen from the paper's model.
- **Input and update:** how the state at step $n$ influences the next state, through which wave-mediated interactions and external drive.
- **Target:** a defined periodic orbit or phase-coherent state, not a normative “good” state.
- **Disturbance:** a registered displacement, phase kick or brief drive perturbation.
- **Correction/recovery metric:** distance from the target orbit over time, recovery probability and/or recovery time.
- **Controls:** reciprocal/equal-scattering conditions where feasible, sham perturbations and matched drive/environment.

If the state-transition map is not meaningfully iterative, or no recovery is observed after the registered perturbation, call the phenomenon a driven/nonreciprocal dynamical system rather than evidence of ARC self-correction. That is a result the programme should be willing to report.

## 5. Operationalising the ARC equation in a physical system

Do not set $U=$ “intelligence” or $R=$ “time” in the physical experiment. Define the physical output first.

For one candidate endpoint, let $Q(N)$ be a preregistered phase-coherence or recovery score derived from the measured trajectory. Fix its normalisation, range, estimator and measurement noise before the confirmatory run. If a power-law model is tested, use a dimensionless cycle ratio $R=N/N_0$, with a declared reference count $N_0$, and state the mapped equation as:

$
\frac{Q(N)}{Q(N_0)} \approx \left(\frac{N}{N_0}\right)^{\alpha_{\mathrm{phys}}}
$

This is an **operationalisation to test**, not a claim that coherence must rise as a power law. A transient may grow, decay, saturate, oscillate or follow a known stability equation. The analysis must include the published system's mechanistic linear-instability/transient account, an appropriate saturation model, and a power-law alternative. The decisive comparison is out-of-sample predictive performance across whole experimental realisations—not which curve looks best when fitted and judged on the same trace.

The equation $U=I R^\alpha$ can be used as shorthand only when the paper explicitly maps $U$ to $Q$, $I$ to the reference output, $R$ to the dimensionless iteration/cycle ratio, and $\alpha$ to the estimated slope. If that mapping is unnatural or fails dimensional and causal checks, do not force it.

### Why alpha = 0.75 must not be imposed on the time crystal

The $d/(d+1)$ expression belongs to a conditional physical-network hypothesis requiring a derivation for the domain's geometry, network and transport assumptions. “The apparatus is three-dimensional” is not such a derivation. The Grier system is a two-particle nonreciprocal oscillator, not automatically a three-dimensional hierarchical transport network. The value 0.75 therefore must not be hard-coded as its predicted exponent unless a separate derivation demonstrates that the required assumptions hold and fixes the measurement before new data are examined.

Likewise, the observed frozen-model estimate near 0.49 is an AI-domain estimate, not a value to transfer into the physical experiment. A true cross-domain claim requires each mapping to be independently justified and the predicted relation between exponents declared before measuring the test domain.

## 6. Recommended staged study

### Stage A — Reanalyse published data (exploratory model discrimination)

**Question:** Does a prespecified ARC-transient model predict the published time-crystal trajectories better out of sample than the standard mechanistic rival?

1. Recover the exact public data, analysis code, metadata and units referenced by the source paper; hash and archive the copies used. If the files or provenance cannot be recovered, stop and report the limitation.
2. Before fitting, freeze the endpoint, trajectory preprocessing, missing-data rules, fit window, allowed model families, model complexity penalties and holdout scheme.
3. Split by entire experimental realisation, never by randomly selected time points from the same trajectory. Use leave-one-realisation-out prediction or a preregistered held-out realisation set.
4. Compare the ARC candidate with the standard linear-instability transient and relevant bounded/saturation alternatives. Report parameter uncertainty, residual diagnostics and predictive performance for every model, including failed fits.
5. Treat AICc/BIC on the full dataset as secondary descriptive analyses only. A curve-selection winner on known data is not a fresh discovery.

The published paper is already public and may have been available when the current draft prediction was formulated. Stage A must therefore be described as a transparent reanalysis, not independent prospective confirmation.

### Stage B — Perturbation and recovery experiment

**Question:** Does the physical system recover toward a specified dynamical state after a controlled disturbance, and how does recovery scale with disturbance and cycle count?

1. Choose one primary disturbance, one primary recovery endpoint and a physically justified range before collection.
2. Randomise perturbation and sham/control conditions within registered blocks. Preserve raw high-speed trajectories and exact timing.
3. Measure phase error, amplitude/order parameter, recovery time and failure to recover; quantify uncertainty over independent runs.
4. Compare nonreciprocal and reciprocal/equal-scattering conditions only where the apparatus supports a valid matched control. Document any parameters that cannot be matched.
5. Test whether a stable restoring/recovery relation exists and whether any ARC-derived relationship predicts held-out runs better than standard dynamics. A stable orbit without recovery after perturbation is not a positive self-correction result.

### Stage C — Mechanical acceleration as a perturbation (not a relativity test)

A controlled translation or rotation can test how inertial forcing changes the apparatus' dynamics. It does **not**, by itself, demonstrate that gravitational curvature, gravitational redshift or relativistic time dilation governs the observed oscillation. Acceleration can change effective forces, orientation, vibration, pressure, temperature and tracking geometry.

If pursued, measure acceleration independently and control those mechanical/environmental effects. Preregister the predicted change in a measured dynamic variable and compare it with ordinary mechanics first. Call this a mechanical acceleration experiment unless it actually measures a relativistic clock effect.

### Stage D — A separate proper-time experiment (only if the theory makes a quantitative prediction)

If a specific ARC/HRIH claim says the recursion metric is coupled to relativistic proper time, it must state the predicted clock observable and magnitude. Compare suitable clocks along defined worldlines or gravitational potentials, model ordinary general-relativistic predictions first, propagate instrument uncertainty, and establish whether the predicted residual is measurable with available equipment. An acoustic oscillation-frequency change is not a clock-comparison result unless environmental and dynamical effects have been excluded at the required precision.

Do not run Stage D until the theory gives a quantitative prediction that differs from established relativity. “Recursion interacts with spacetime” alone is too broad to decide an experiment.

## 7. The pre-registration gate and falsification conditions

The current dated register lists study-t as **draft (working)**. Maintain that status until the author has approved the final protocol and completed the documented registration/submission steps. Do not change the register merely because this planning document exists.

Before a new-data confirmatory study, freeze and timestamp:

- the exact research question and which proposition it informs;
- the physical meaning of $Q$, $N$, $N_0$, $\tau$, and every other symbol;
- the apparatus, treatment/control conditions, exclusions, sample unit and stopping rule;
- primary model families, fitting windows, priors/constraints if any, and the out-of-sample scoring rule;
- sample-size or precision justification based on simulation of the complete scoring pipeline;
- the quantitative prediction interval or minimum effect that counts as support;
- the conditions that count as a refutation, an ambiguous result, missing data or a failed instrument;
- data, code, calibration, version and provenance requirements; and
- independent replication responsibilities and publication of null results.

**Prospective falsification logic (the exact numerical thresholds must be set by the author before registration):**

| Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| The standard linear-instability model equals or exceeds the ARC model on held-out realisations | No added predictive support for the ARC transient in this dataset |
| The preregistered ARC model loses its predicted super-linear transient, after measurement and preprocessing checks pass | The specific transient prediction is falsified for the tested conditions |
| A model-family preference changes under modest preregistered, scientifically defensible preprocessing choices | The claimed physical form is not robust at the current measurement quality |
| The system oscillates but does not recover after the registered disturbance | Sustained oscillation is observed; self-correction is not supported by this test |
| An independently operated new experiment reproduces the preregistered effect and beats the standard rival out of sample | Evidence for the specific physical-domain prediction; still not proof of a universal ARC law or cosmological hypothesis |
| Results are null, imprecise or underpowered | Report as null/undecided, not as confirmation by analogy |

Do not call 6,700 cycles a collapse threshold. Paper III's current time-crystal discussion associates roughly 100 seconds / roughly 6,700 oscillations with a reported coherence duration. A measured duration is a reference observation; it is not automatically a predicted failure point, a universal maximum recursion depth or a kill condition.

## 8. Cauchy Unification and physical scaling: next test beyond study-t

Paper VII's valid mathematical core is conditional: specified composition equations and regularity conditions constrain admissible forms. It does not make every empirical process a homomorphism, and the current text leaves the exhaustiveness of the bounded/saturation case open. Preserve the four relevant functional-equation cells and distinguish strictly Cauchy-derived cases from compatible but not fully derived saturation forms.

For a genuine physical scaling test:

1. Name the physical observable, its units, the process variable and the mechanism that fixes the composition operator.
2. Fix the operator classification without showing the analyst the outcome curves, where a blind classification is feasible.
3. Set the admissible candidate models before fitting, including reasonable domain-specific physical rivals; avoid selecting only models that match ARC.
4. Use new or genuinely held-out domains for confirmatory evaluation. If a domain informed the hypothesis, label its fit exploratory or retrospective.
5. Report per-domain results, failed fits, uncertainty, dependence between datasets and sensitivity to the candidate set. Do not combine identities, reference values, retrospective matches and independent empirical tests into one “validation” number.
6. Test $d/(d+1)$ only in systems whose network geometry and assumptions derive it. Do not treat the spatial dimension alone as explanatory evidence.
7. For cosmology, derive the predicted scale-factor behaviour from the specified cosmological model and compare it with established Friedmann solutions and observational uncertainties. A shared exponent is suggestive, not proof of a shared recursive mechanism.

## 9. Connection to Recursive Creation / HRIH

Keep the name and the hypothesis. Do not dilute it to avoid a first impression, but make the epistemic contract explicit at first reading:

- **Established physics:** relativistic spacetime, measurable proper-time differences, gravitational waves, and thermodynamic constraints on physical computation.
- **Mathematical result:** what follows from the explicit axioms in the current ARC/Cauchy/Paper X derivations.
- **Empirical finding:** what a named dataset and code actually measured, with the same limitations as the source.
- **Cross-domain hypothesis:** the predicted correspondence between these separately measured systems.
- **Cosmological interpretation:** the additional HRIH/Recursive Creation proposition that a higher-dimensional or timeless substrate generates universes and that intelligence could eventually participate in that process.

Higher-dimensional spaces are serious mathematical and theoretical-physics objects; that fact alone does not establish a timeless substrate outside our spacetime or a creator process. A credible HRIH test must identify its model space, geometry/metric (if applicable), rules of causal or informational composition, connection to observable 3+1-dimensional quantities, and a result that would differ from competing cosmological models. If it has no distinctive observable consequence yet, label it philosophical/speculative, rather than saying that established physics proves it.

The sentence “time exists because minds cannot be grown without it” can remain as the thesis's philosophical interpretation. It should not be presented as the conclusion of relativity, the entropy law, or $U=I R^\alpha$ unless a separate derivation supplies the missing steps.

## 10. Suggested research-programme wording

> **Physical Recursion and Spacetime — a falsifiable research question.** The ARC programme asks whether some systems that exhibit iterative feedback can be described by related scaling forms across computational and physical domains. Its physical programme begins with a narrow test: whether a prespecified model of feedback, recovery and saturation predicts independent acoustic time-crystal trajectories better than the established dynamical alternative. This is not a claim that a time crystal is an AI, that cycle count is elapsed time, or that the oscillator measures gravitational time dilation. Results will be evaluated against the physical model first; any broader claim about a shared recursive law or its cosmological implications requires separately registered, independent tests.
