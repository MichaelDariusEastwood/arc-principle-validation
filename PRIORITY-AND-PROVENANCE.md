## How the files are organised (so nothing is misread)

- **Canonical files carry no version number in the filename.** Each document has one canonical name; its full history is the *Revisions* tab on that file.
- **The version stack IS the history.** Re-uploading under the same name adds a new version on top; it never overwrites the earlier one. Example: `Executive-Summary.pdf` holds four versions, v1 dated 17 Mar 2026, latest 4 Jul 2026.
- **The `archive/` folder** holds superseded, separately-named earlier drafts, kept for the record with their own original March 2026 dates. Historical copies, not the primary priority record.
- **Nothing is ever deleted.** Superseded material is moved to `archive/`, never removed.

## What priority is, and is not, claimed

Priority is claimed only for what the anchored artifacts actually contain on their timestamped
dates. Where independent researchers reached related results, priority is credited to them in
Paper XI wherever their dates require it; several convergences are logged as concurrent or later
than the external work, not as claims against it. This document establishes when this author's own
artifacts existed; it does not assert that no one else has reasoned about recursion, correction, or scaling.
