WHAT THIS IS
This is the registered text of a theory-level registration: twenty-two predictions about
systems that improve themselves, and in particular about whether the correction that
holds such a system to its specification can keep pace as its capability rises. Each
prediction is stated with the circumstances it is claimed to hold under, the observation
that would show it false, what falls with it if it fails, whether any instrument exists
today that could decide it, and the author's confidence or his refusal to give one. The
wording was fixed on a public registry before any outcome was examined against it.
Nothing here is a result. This is a registration record and is not a paper; it is not
counted in the paper series.
