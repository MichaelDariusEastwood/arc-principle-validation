Abstract
--------
The ARC/Eden research programme now spans a full set of documents and a run of
independent empirical studies covering mathematical foundations, methodology,
empirical validation, engineering specification, and philosophical vision. The
core concepts were first articulated on 8 December 2024 in an email bearing a
visible Gmail origination field, with sender-DKIM and paired Google ARC
mathematics verifying against keys captured at the signed selectors and no RFC
3161 trusted timestamp. The book Infinite Architects (ISBN 978-1806056200) was
published 2 January 2026 (print; ebook 6 January). The research programme was
produced between February and March 2026. This synthesis paper integrates all
findings into a single honest assessment. Across the empirical studies: 1
produced a clear positive result (gated simulation, the only experiment using
deterministic metrics on a system without pre-existing RLHF training), 2
produced null results (DGM v3, where RLHF-trained models resisted prompt-level
differentiation), 1 produced inconclusive results (weight-level LoRA
fine-tuning, where instruct-tuned models were too strong for LoRA to override),
and the remainder produced statistically significant findings under blinded
evaluation. The $d/(d+1)$ geometric scaling bound, proposed as an invariant
relation under test, matches observed scaling across 50 domains from mice to
galaxies, and Cauchy functional equations fix the functional form once the
composition identity and a regularity condition are given, and saturation is
admitted as a fourth, capped case rather than as a solution of those equations.
This paper maps exactly what has been proven, what has not, and what experiments
would resolve each open question. It also documents the programme's errors and
corrections, because a framework built on iterative self-correction must
practise what it preaches.
