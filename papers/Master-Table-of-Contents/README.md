## Summary

From the abstract (version 3.34):

> This table of contents maps every document of the programme in which its author founded Recursive Dynamics, the study of what limits systems that improve themselves. The programme asks what would keep a self-improving AI system aligned and useful if control of it were lost against our will, and it holds that control, hardware included, should be kept for as long as it physically can be. Its founding theory, the ARC Theory, states five laws, each a law under test. Their foundation is Law I, the ARC Principle, which gives capability as a power of recursive depth, $U = I R^{\alpha}$, and grew from the equation $U = IR$ written on 8 December 2024. The Eden Protocol specifies loops of ethical evaluation and self-correction built into a system’s architecture before training, run at each defined step of reasoning and action and taking part in every rewrite. Its governance proposal, the HARI Treaty, the Draft Treaty on Hardware-Aligned Recursive Intelligence, is a draft for consultation that no State has negotiated, adopted or signed. The programme holds thirty-six documents: twelve in theory, ten in empirical work, three in engineering, two in governance, six overviews and records and three in other fields. Each has a card giving its area and type, its version and date and its DOI, and saying in this document’s own words what it asks and what it found, at the grade it gives itself. A reading path, a dated timeline, the main results, a glossary, the counts and the citations follow, and the status of the laws, with the one registered verdict so far, is stated once, in Section 9. Every document is listed at the version public at its DOI on 26 November 2026, and where a card and its document differ, the document governs.
