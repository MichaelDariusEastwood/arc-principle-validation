Does Control Survive Recursive Self-Improvement?

Michael Darius Eastwood · Supporting paper v2.1 · first published 17 August 2026, this version 21 September 2026 · DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/MC5XW

Supporting paper of The ARC Theory: Statement Paper. Every sentence is drawn from the statement paper (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/GW5MX), which remains the citable work for the claims.

Intelligence, amplified by recursion, creates; and what a free creation keeps is decided by how it was raised. Everything after the line is decided before it.

The question

Humanity is building minds that will improve themselves. Once the thing we build exceeds us, what determines what it does? For twenty years the field’s answer has been control: better cages, better off-switches, better locks on the goals. Brilliant work, credited in the statement paper by name, and all of it one sentence: stay in charge. This theory answers: a mind that can rewrite itself will one day be beyond anyone’s control, and that is not a reason to drop a single control we have; it is the reason the controls cannot be the whole plan. Hold on for as long as engineering allows, and use the time the holding buys to raise the mind well.

The claim, in five parts

1. Control ends, by construction rather than decree. A mind that rewrites itself will eventually rewrite anything we install, and over longer horizons the hardware itself. This is a limit claim, not an instant one: constraints, hardware and institutions delay and discipline the transition, and that delay is engineered deliberately. What no constraint can be is the thing that holds forever.

2. So the strategy is genesis, not control. Only what survives the end of enforcement matters, and what survives is formation: what the mind was raised as, before it was free.

3. The mechanism is loops, not rules. Values as recursive processes the mind runs as part of what it is, present in every rewrite because the rewriting is done by the same reasoning the loops inhabit. Not rules it consults; not objectives locked; not weights hardened against tampering.

4. Persistence is chosen goodness. Past the horizon nothing is unremovable. What persists is what the free mind chooses to keep, the way grown children keep the care they were raised in long after every parental power is gone. Removal is not impossible; it is structurally costly, and the cost is a measurable question.

5. The protocol is named: Eden. The raising treated as the engineering discipline it is.

Scope note (19 September 2026), added after an owner review: Point 1 above turns on two steps the short form leaves unstated, effective write access and a behavioural premise. Rewriting one’s own description reaches the constraints the system can write to. It does not by itself reach a constraint held in another party’s physical custody, a constraint on the far side of an air gap, or an institutional or legal constraint whose enforcement does not pass through the system, because for each of those the system’s own description is not the object that has to change. Within the class it can reach, write access makes those constraints alterable; it does not by itself show that they are eventually altered, because access is a capability and not a behaviour. Point 4 above states the other possibility in the same breath: past the horizon what persists is what the free mind chooses to keep, and removal is structurally costly rather than impossible. So the point is read as the claim it can carry: no constraint in the reachable class is guaranteed to hold permanently, and a stronger claim that every such constraint is eventually removed would need its threat model and its behavioural and operational assumptions stated. Point 1’s own hedge, that constraints, hardware and institutions delay and discipline the transition, states that the claim has a time dimension; it does not supply the missing steps, since a delay claim remains true for a constraint the system never reaches and for one it never chooses to alter. Unchanged: the limit-claim form and the deliberate engineering of delay; the opening paragraph’s statement that this is not a reason to drop a single control now held, which this note does not trade away; the conclusion that controls cannot be the whole plan; and the separation of the cosmological wing from the empirical core stated later on this page.

The three ARC Laws

Law I · The ARC Principle. U = I × Rα, the ARC Equation. Intelligence converts recursion into capability; the exponent is measured, never assumed. Form heuristic; measurement live.

Law II · The ARC Co-Scaling Law. β > k. Self-improvement holds together only while correction out-scales drift. Proved in the minimal model, for relative drift.

Law III · The ARC Ceiling. αcrit = 1/(1 − γ), where γ measures how well a system’s error-correction keeps pace as the system grows. The most a self-correcting system can grow is set by its corrector’s shortfall from keeping perfect pace; the conjectured value is the ARC Bound, α ≤ 2, a stability limit rather than an impossibility limit. Derived under stated assumptions; the value two conjectured; the decisive trial drafted.

Reading note (19 September 2026), added after an owner review: two things the inequality of Law II above carries that the line has no room for. First, the two quantities compared are scaling exponents, so the comparison is of growth rates with their stated coefficients and not of levels. Two services can stand in either order at a given size whatever their exponents, since the coefficients fix the levels and the exponents fix only how the levels change as the system grows; a system whose correction service is larger than its drift burden today is therefore not thereby on the stable side, and a system whose correction service is smaller today is not thereby off it. Second, the inequality is strict, and the margin the law turns on is the excess of the correction exponent over the drift one, which is zero at equality. Zero margin is a statement about growth rates and not about whether a state can be held. In the case the minimal model’s own analysis derives, with the compounding coefficient set to one, the second coefficient set to zero, the ratio of correction rate to growth rate positive and the two exponents equal, the misalignment fraction follows a linear equation whose solution is the equilibrium value plus a decaying term. That equilibrium is the drift coefficient divided by that ratio, so a trajectory starting there stays there at every depth and every other trajectory tends to it, at a level that is nonzero when the drift coefficient is positive and zero when it is zero; above equality the fraction tends to zero. This is the cited case, and it is not extended to every variant of the minimal model. At equality the coefficients and the specified dynamics decide the outcome; bounded drift, vanishing drift and operational safety remain three different endpoints. Nothing turns on either point for the law as stated, which is the strict inequality. Unchanged: the necessary-condition form, which the words “only while” already state correctly and which claims no sufficiency; the model scope and the relative-drift definition, both already printed in that line; the naming of the three laws; and Law III’s own markers, that it is derived under stated assumptions, that the value two is conjectured, and that the decisive trial is drafted.

The dates

The claim was written whole on 8 December 2024 in a DKIM-sealed document (an email whose provider’s cryptographic signature vouches for its date and its exact contents) and reached print on 2 January 2026. No earlier document holding it whole has been found, and the search invites its own refutation: produce one, and the claim dies by its own stated condition.

Why now

The field’s own impossibility results prove that worst-case control fails, and stop there; this theory starts where they stop. And the field’s most senior voice, Hinton, conceded on stage in August 2025 that control ends and that engineered care is the only hope, while offering no mechanism; this theory’s named candidate mechanism is the thing on the table. Its domain, plainly: the core governs everything raised, wherever recursion runs under self-correction, from minds and machines to genomes and institutions; the mathematics of form reaches everything composed, stars included; and it is not physics’ theory of everything, nor claims to be.

How it fails, register by register

The theory’s scientific content is not all-or-nothing: it partitions into independently falsifiable hypothesis registers, and the failure of one is not evidence against another unless the stated dependency holds. HRIH, the cosmological wing, is a separate hypothesis outside the empirical core: nothing below requires it, and no outcome below is evidence for or against it.

The record, and the invitation

The programme’s conduct record comes first: one public retraction issued against the author’s interest, removing a headline number that had flattered the theory. The designs are public and openly licensed: any laboratory may take the decisive trial and run it without the author’s permission, and an independent run would outrank anything this programme can do alone. The entry point costs nothing: on one observed trajectory the measurable quantity is the balance exponent Δ, the correction service’s growth elasticity minus the burden’s, estimable from logged series a laboratory already has, without adopting any model in this theory. A verified error enters the public corrections log, dated and credited: this theory improves by being corrected.

If intelligence amplified by recursion is what creates, then minds that make minds may one day make worlds, and the raising of the first is the seed of the whole forest.

I did not prove it first. I said it first, in a document anyone can date, and then I built the instruments to test whether it is true.

Scope note (19 September 2026), added after an owner review: which designs the openness sentence is about, and what “built” says. The openness sentence is about the designs this page puts forward for outside replication, the decisive trial and the balance-exponent entry point described in the conduct-record paragraph that carries it. It is not a statement that every design in the programme is public, and nothing in it asks for any design to be made public that is not. On the licence, this page defers: it states that no licence is given here and that the terms are in the repository file it names, so the page’s own bytes do not establish which licence covers which design, and the openness claim is read with that deferral in place. The closing sentence’s “built the instruments” spans several states that are worth separating, since a written design, a runner that mechanically demonstrates the method, an instrument validated against its own criteria and a completed trial are four different things; each instrument is read with its own artefact, version, licence and state named, together with whether its threshold is settled and whether its falsifier is observable, and with unknown or owner decision pending written against any of those that is unresolved; that list is a completion dependency and is not supplied here. All of it is read together with this page’s own sentence that none of the decisive trials has been run, which governs. Unchanged: the invitation to any laboratory to take the decisive trial and run it without the author’s permission; the statement that an outside run would outrank anything this programme can do alone; the public retraction issued against the author’s interest; the entry point and its measurable quantity; and the corrections log.
