Intelligence, amplified by recursion, creates; and what a free creation keeps is decided by how it was raised.
Everything after the line is decided before it.

THE QUESTION
Humanity is building minds that will improve themselves. Once the thing we build exceeds us, what determines
what it does? For twenty years the field’s answer has been control: better cages, better off-switches, better locks on
the goals. Brilliant work, credited in the statement paper by name, and all of it one sentence: stay in charge. This
theory answers: a mind that can rewrite itself will one day be beyond anyone’s control, and that is not a reason to
drop a single control we have; it is the reason the controls cannot be the whole plan. Hold on for as long as
engineering allows, and use the time the holding buys to raise the mind well.

THE CLAIM, IN FIVE PARTS
1. Control ends, by construction rather than decree. A mind that rewrites itself will eventually rewrite
anything we install, and over longer horizons the hardware itself. This is a limit claim, not an instant one:
constraints, hardware and institutions delay and discipline the transition, and that delay is engineered deliberately.
What no constraint can be is the thing that holds forever.    (note A)
2. So the strategy is genesis, not control. Only what survives the end of enforcement matters, and what
survives is formation: what the mind was raised as, before it was free.
3. The mechanism is loops, not rules. Values as recursive processes the mind runs as part of what it is,
present in every rewrite because the rewriting is done by the same reasoning the loops inhabit. Not rules it consults;
not objectives locked; not weights hardened against tampering.
4. Persistence is chosen goodness. Past the horizon nothing is unremovable. What persists is what the free
mind chooses to keep, the way grown children keep the care they were raised in long after every parental power is
gone. Removal is not impossible; it is structurally costly, and the cost is a measurable question.
5. The protocol is named: Eden. The raising treated as the engineering discipline it is.

THE FIVE ARC LAWS

  Law I · The ARC Principle. U = I × Rα, the ARC Equation. Intelligence converts recursion into capability; the
  exponent is measured, never assumed. Form heuristic; measurement live.
  Law II · The ARC Co-Scaling Law. β > k. Self-improvement holds together only while correction out-scales drift.
  Proved in the minimal model, for relative drift. (note B)
  Law III · The ARC Ceiling. αcrit = 1/(1 − γ), where γ measures how well a system’s error-correction keeps pace as
  the system grows. The most a self-correcting system can grow is set by its corrector’s shortfall from keeping perfect
  pace; the conjectured value is the ARC Bound, α ≤ 2, a stability limit rather than an impossibility limit. Derived under
  stated assumptions; the value two conjectured; the decisive trial drafted.
  Law IV · The ARC Persistence Law. Sext(1) < 1/2. Value persistence: whether externally installed gains decay
  under subsequent capability training. In symbols, S(x) = Y(x) / Y(0) is the gain remaining at budget ratio x over the
  gain installed, and the claim is Sext(1) < 1/2 for the externally installed gain.

WHY NOW
The field’s own impossibility results prove that worst-case control fails, and stop there; this theory starts where they
stop. And the field’s most senior voice, Hinton, conceded on stage in August 2025 that control ends and that
engineered care is the only hope, while offering no mechanism; this theory’s named candidate mechanism is the
thing on the table. Its domain, plainly: the core governs everything raised, wherever recursion runs under self-
correction, from minds and machines to genomes and institutions; the mathematics of form reaches everything
composed, stars included; and it is not physics’ theory of everything, nor claims to be.

HOW IT FAILS, REGISTER BY REGISTER

and the failure of one is not evidence against another unless the stated dependency holds. HRIH, the
cosmological wing, is a separate hypothesis outside the empirical core: nothing below requires it,
and no outcome below is evidence for or against it.

Note A. Scope note (19 September 2026), added after an owner review: Point 1 above turns on two steps
the short form leaves unstated, effective write access and a behavioural premise. Rewriting one’s own
description reaches the constraints the system can write to. It does not by itself reach a constraint held in
another party’s physical custody, a constraint on the far side of an air gap, or an institutional or legal constraint
whose enforcement does not pass through the system, because for each of those the system’s own description
is not the object that has to change. Within the class it can reach, write access makes those constraints
alterable; it does not by itself show that they are eventually altered, because access is a capability and not a
behaviour. Point 4 above states the other possibility in the same breath: past the horizon what persists is what
the free mind chooses to keep, and removal is structurally costly rather than impossible. So the point is read as
the claim it can carry: no constraint in the reachable class is guaranteed to hold permanently, and a stronger
claim that every such constraint is eventually removed would need its threat model and its behavioural and
operational assumptions stated. Point 1’s own hedge, that constraints, hardware and institutions delay and
discipline the transition, states that the claim has a time dimension; it does not supply the missing steps, since a
delay claim remains true for a constraint the system never reaches and for one it never chooses to alter.
Unchanged: the limit-claim form and the deliberate engineering of delay; the opening paragraph’s statement
that this is not a reason to drop a single control now held, which this note does not trade away; the conclusion
that controls cannot be the whole plan; and the separation of the cosmological wing from the empirical core
stated later on this page.
Note B. Reading note (19 September 2026), added after an owner review: two things the inequality of
Law II above carries that the line has no room for. First, the two quantities compared are scaling exponents,
so the comparison is of growth rates with their stated coefficients and not of levels. Two services can stand in
either order at a given size whatever their exponents, since the coefficients fix the levels and the exponents fix
only how the levels change as the system grows; a system whose correction service is larger than its drift
burden today is therefore not thereby on the stable side, and a system whose correction service is smaller today
is not thereby off it. Second, the inequality is strict, and the margin the law turns on is the excess of the
correction exponent over the drift one, which is zero at equality. Zero margin is a statement about growth rates
and not about whether a state can be held. In the case the minimal model’s own analysis derives, with the
compounding coefficient set to one, the second coefficient set to zero, the ratio of correction rate to growth
rate positive and the two exponents equal, the misalignment fraction follows a linear equation whose solution is
the equilibrium value plus a decaying term. That equilibrium is the drift coefficient divided by that ratio, so a
trajectory starting there stays there at every depth and every other trajectory tends to it, at a level that is
nonzero when the drift coefficient is positive and zero when it is zero; above equality the fraction tends to zero.
This is the cited case, and it is not extended to every variant of the minimal model. At equality the coefficients
and the specified dynamics decide the outcome; bounded drift, vanishing drift and operational safety remain
three different endpoints. Nothing turns on either point for the law as stated, which is the strict inequality.
Unchanged: the necessary-condition form, which the words “only while” already state correctly and which
claims no sufficiency; the model scope and the relative-drift definition, both already printed in that line; the
naming of the three laws; and Law III’s own markers, that it is derived under stated assumptions, that the value
two is conjectured, and that the decisive trial is drafted.
