## Summary

From the abstract (version 3.5):

> Humanity is building minds that will improve themselves. Once such a mind exceeds us, what determines what it does? This form states the ARC Theory, the founding theory of Recursive Dynamics, on two printed pages. Its claim has five parts, in the words of the dated record: control ends, by construction rather than decree; so the strategy is genesis, not control; the mechanism is loops, not rules; persistence is chosen goodness; and the protocol is named: Eden. That is not a reason to drop a single control we have; it is the reason the controls cannot be the whole plan, and control is held for as long as engineering allows. Each of the five laws is a law under test. Law I, the ARC Principle, $U = I \times R^{\alpha}$, is a form heuristic, its exponent to be measured, never assumed. Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law, $\beta > k$, is proved for positive gain drift in a minimal model, for relative drift. Law III, the ARC Ceiling, $\alpha_{\mathrm{crit}} = 1/(1-\gamma)$, is derived under stated assumptions, its value two, the ARC Bound, conjectured. Laws IV and V, the ARC Persistence Law and the ARC Embedding Law, were registered as laws on 15 October 2026; Law IV is not yet tested. Unit 83, the programme's first registered study to reach a verdict, tested P21 under Law V: P21 is supported under the registered test, by the programme's own run, on the access axis, family-dependent, with its mechanism axis not evaluable and one call retried under a disclosed recovery amendment. The form gives the dated record of the claim (written whole on 8 December 2024 and in print on 2 January 2026), what would refute each of the theory's five registers and what falls with it, the programme's public retraction, and an entry point a laboratory can estimate from logged series it already has. The Statement Paper (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/GW5MX) remains the citable work for the claims.
