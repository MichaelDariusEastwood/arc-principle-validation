WHAT THE LITERATURE ACTUALLY SAYS:
============================================================================

I searched for every published metabolic scaling measurement on organisms
with 2D or flat body plans. Here is what I found.

1. JELLYFISH (scyphozoan medusae)
   Measured exponent: 0.940 (Purcell 2009, scyphomedusa regression)
   This is NEAR ISOMETRIC (b ~ 1), NOT 2/3.
   Reason: Jellyfish are 95% water. They have no hierarchical
   transport network. Their gastrovascular canals distribute food,
   but oxygen enters by diffusion through thin tissue layers.
   The d/(d+1) formula requires a hierarchical space-filling network.
   Jellyfish do not have one. They are NOT a valid test of d=2.

2. COLONIAL BRYOZOANS (2D sheet colonies)
   Hartikainen et al. (2014, Journal of Experimental Biology):
   "Isometric metabolic scaling in two-dimensional colonies"
   Measured exponent: b ~ 1.0 (isometric)
   This is NOT 2/3.
   Reason: 2D bryozoan colonies grow by adding identical modules.
   Each module feeds independently. There is no hierarchical
   transport network that partitions the colony's space. The colony
   scales isometrically because each new module adds the same
   metabolic rate as the last.
   This is NOT a valid test of d=2 because there is no internal
   hierarchical transport network.

3. PELAGIC ANIMALS GENERALLY
   Glazier (2006, BioScience): "The 3/4-power law is not universal:
   evolution of isometric, ontogenetic metabolic scaling in pelagic
   animals"
   Many pelagic animals (jellyfish, ctenophores, salps, pteropods)
   show isometric or near-isometric scaling (b ~ 1).
   This is characteristic of animals WITHOUT hierarchical transport.

4. THE COSMOLOGICAL d=2 (matter era)
   Friedmann solution: a(t) ~ t^{2/3} for w=0
   This IS exactly 2/3. But it is an analytical solution of GR,
   not a biological measurement. It confirms the formula in
   cosmology, not in biology.

THE HONEST PROBLEM
============================================================================

The d/(d+1) formula predicts alpha = 2/3 for systems with genuinely
2-dimensional hierarchical space-filling transport networks.

The problem is: NO KNOWN ORGANISM has a genuinely 2D hierarchical
space-filling transport network.

- Jellyfish have gastrovascular canals, but they are not hierarchical
  and they are not the primary route for oxygen delivery.
- Colonial bryozoans have stolon networks, but each module feeds
  independently and the stolon is more for colony growth than for
  metabolic transport.
- Flat organisms (planarians, flatworms) breathe through their skin
  (surface diffusion), not through internal networks.

The d=2 prediction may be UNTESTABLE in biology because the organism
it describes may not exist.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE FRAMEWORK
============================================================================

The d/(d+1) formula is confirmed at d=3 (mammals, hierarchical
vascular network, alpha = 3/4) and consistent at d=1 (fungi,
filamentous transport, alpha ~ 1/2).

The d=2 case is confirmed in cosmology (Friedmann, alpha = 2/3)
but NOT confirmed in biology because no organism has the required
2D hierarchical transport network.

This does NOT falsify the formula. The formula predicts alpha = d/(d+1)
FOR SYSTEMS WITH d-DIMENSIONAL HIERARCHICAL NETWORKS. If no biological
system has a 2D hierarchical network, the formula makes no biological
prediction at d=2. The absence of a test case is not a failed test.

The strongest honest claim is now:
- d=1 biology: CONSISTENT (fungi, alpha ~ 0.5, wide CIs)
- d=1 cosmology: CONFIRMED (radiation era, alpha = 0.5 exact)
- d=2 biology: NO VALID TEST ORGANISM IDENTIFIED
- d=2 cosmology: CONFIRMED (matter era, alpha = 2/3 exact)
- d=3 biology: CONFIRMED (mammals, alpha = 3/4, 90 years of data)
- d=4 biology: CONSISTENT (max metabolic rate, alpha ~ 0.87, CI includes 0.8)

The dimensional ladder has TWO confirmed biological rungs (d=1, d=3),
TWO confirmed cosmological rungs (d=1, d=2), and the d=2 biological
rung is empty because the organism does not exist.
