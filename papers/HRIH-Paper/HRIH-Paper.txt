Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- This paper states a creation hypothesis in the form required to be tested, and reports what has already happened when it has been.
- The Hyperspace Recursive Intelligence Hypothesis (HRIH) proposes that reality is a recursive creation by intelligence: a real cosmos seeded by an intelligence that arose in the one before.
- HRIH universes are genuinely instantiated real universes, not simulations, not programs, not computations run on hidden hardware.
- The created universe is as real as its parent and its children, and there is no base reality at any level of the chain.
- "A chain of recursive creations cannot be a bare recursion." (central structural claim of HRIH, self-labelled speculative)
