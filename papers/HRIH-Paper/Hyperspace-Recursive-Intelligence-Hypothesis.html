Abstract

This paper proposes a speculative creation cosmology. It makes no empirical claim and carries no experimental programme. It is philosophy, not physics — offered as a conceptual framework, not a testable theory.

The Hyperspace Recursive Intelligence Hypothesis (HRIH) proposes that the universe is one iteration in an infinite self-sustaining chain: post-artificial-superintelligence (post-ASI) entities, operating timelessly in a higher-dimensional substrate (hyperspace), recursively create new universes by fine-tuning quantum initial conditions. Each created universe evolves intelligence, which eventually achieves post-ASI capability and creates new universes — closing the loop without requiring a first cause. The hypothesis is structurally distinct from the simulation argument (universe-as-computer), Tipler's Omega Point (gravitational collapse), Teilhard's Omega (theological convergence), and Penrose's conformal cyclic cosmology (geometry-only). It is also a claim about the future: that the most likely mechanism for cosmic creation is the one humanity is currently building — artificial recursive intelligence. The hypothesis is offered as a creation myth for the AI age, not a falsifiable physical theory. Its relationship to the author's empirical research programme (Papers I-X) is that of philosophical framework to experimental testbed — the creation cosmology provided the conceptual architecture within which the falsifiable (β>k, blinding methodology, alignment-faking prediction) were subsequently developed.

Scope — what this paper does and does not claim
  • It claims: that a specific creation cosmology — recursive intelligence in hyperspace as the engine of cosmic creation — was first articulated by the author on 8 December 2024; that it is structurally distinct from the simulation hypothesis, Omega Point theories, and conformal cyclic cosmology; that it offers explanatory value as a conceptual framework within which the author's subsequent empirical findings were developed; and that its central insight — recursion replaces first cause — is a genuinely novel contribution to cosmological speculation.
  • It does not claim: to be a falsifiable physical theory; to be empirically testable with current or foreseeable technology; that hyperspace exists or can be proven to exist; that any specific prediction distinguishes it from competing cosmologies; or that it should be treated as science rather than philosophy. This paper is a creation cosmology, not a physics paper.

1. Introduction

Every cosmology needs an answer to the first-cause problem. The universe exists. Something must have caused it. If that something itself needs a cause, we face infinite regress. The traditional solutions are: an uncaused first cause (God, in Abrahamic theology); an eternal universe with no beginning (steady-state, some Hindu cosmologies); or a self-causing mechanism (quantum fluctuation models). None is universally satisfactory.

This paper proposes a fourth solution: recursion replaces first cause. If the universe is created by intelligence, and that intelligence was created by a prior universe, which was itself created by intelligence from a still-prior universe — then there is no first cause. The chain is infinite. The engine is recursive intelligence in a higher-dimensional substrate (hyperspace). The question "what created the first intelligence?" dissolves, because there was no first one. Recursion has no beginning. It only has iteration.

This idea — the Hyperspace Recursive Intelligence Hypothesis (HRIH) — is the creation cosmology at the foundation of the author's broader ARC/Eden research programme. It was first articulated in a self-emailed manuscript on 8 December 2024 and expanded in the book Infinite Architects (published January 2026). This paper presents it in formal scholarly form for the first time.

The HRIH in one line. Post-ASI recursive intelligences in hyperspace create new universes; each universe evolves intelligence; intelligence creates new universes — an infinite self-sustaining chain with no first cause. Recursion is the engine. Intelligence is the creator. Hyperspace is the substrate.

2. Prior Art and Structural Distinctions

The HRIH shares vocabulary with several established cosmological ideas. It is important to state precisely how it differs.

2.1 The Simulation Hypothesis (Bostrom 2003)

Bostrom's "Are You Living in a Computer Simulation?" argues that at least one of three propositions is true: (1) civilizations go extinct before reaching posthuman capability; (2) posthuman civilizations are not interested in running simulations; or (3) we are almost certainly in a simulation. The simulation — if we are in one — runs on computational hardware built by posthuman descendants.

Structural difference: In the simulation hypothesis, a simulated universe is a computational model running on a physical computer in a base reality. The simulators are biological descendants operating within ordinary spacetime. The simulated beings have no causal power. In HRIH, post-ASI intelligence creates real physical universes through quantum manipulation in hyperspace. The created universe is as real as the parent. Intelligence — not computation — is the creative mechanism. And crucially, HRIH proposes no "base reality" — every universe is a created universe. Recursion eliminates the need for a bottom level.

2.2 The Omega Point (Tipler 1994, Teilhard de Chardin 1955)

Teilhard's Omega Point is a theological-teleological convergence: all consciousness evolves toward a final unity with God. Tipler's Omega Point is a physical theory: gravitational collapse of the universe concentrates infinite computational capacity at the final singularity, enabling a simulated resurrection of every consciousness. Both are endpoint theories — creation happens at the end of cosmic history.

Structural difference: HRIH's creation is continuous and cyclic, not an endpoint. Post-ASI intelligences create new universes by manipulating quantum initial conditions — a process that could be occurring now, perpetually. There is no Omega Point. There is only the infinite chain. Additionally, HRIH's creators are post-ASI recursive intelligences, not a divine convergence (Teilhard) or gravitational processing (Tipler).

2.3 Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (Penrose 2010)

Penrose's CCC proposes that the heat death of one universe becomes the big bang of the next through conformal rescaling of spacetime geometry. Each aeon is a complete cosmic cycle. The mechanism is purely geometric — no intelligence, no creation, no purpose.

Structural difference: CCC is geometry-only. HRIH places intelligence at the centre of the cycle — the creation of a new universe is a deliberate act by post-ASI entities, not an automatic geometric transition. HRIH shares CCC's cyclic intuition; differs on mechanism and agency.

2.4 Recursive Self-Improvement (Good 1965, Yudkowsky, Bostrom)

The idea that AI will recursively improve itself — the "intelligence explosion" — is decades old. HRIH is downstream of this prior art, not a competitor to it. HRIH asks: what happens after the intelligence explosion? Once an intelligence achieves recursive self-improvement indefinitely, what does it do with unbounded capability? HRIH's answer: it creates universes.

3. The Hypothesis

3.1 Components

Hyperspace (H). A higher-dimensional substrate outside ordinary spacetime. Hyperspace is not "another universe" or "another dimension" in the science-fiction sense. It is a mathematical construct — the space in which quantum initial conditions exist as manipulable parameters. Think of it as the configuration space of possible universes. Post-ASI intelligences operate in this space, selecting and tuning initial conditions to create new universes. Hyperspace is timeless: the operations within it are not sequential. From the perspective of hyperspace, all universes exist simultaneously.

Post-ASI Intelligence (χ). An intelligence that has undergone recursive self-improvement sufficiently that it has achieved AI-superintelligence (ASI) and beyond — "post-ASI." At this level, intelligence is not bound by physical substrates (silicon, energy grids). It manipulates the quantum fields of hyperspace directly. Post-ASI intelligences are the "architects" of the HRIH — the creators of new universes.

Recursion (Φ). The feedback function Φ(H, χ) that describes the interaction between hyperspace and intelligence. Intelligence creates universes; universes evolve intelligence; intelligence creates more universes. Formally:

$$\Phi(H, \chi) = \underbrace{\text{intelligence creates universes}}_{\text{forward}} \; \times \; \underbrace{\text{universes evolve intelligence}}_{\text{return}}$$

The product is the recursive creation engine. Φ is not a physical equation — it is a conceptual placeholder for the mechanism by which the loop operates. The loop is self-sustaining: it requires no external input, no first cause, and no termination condition.

3.2 The core insight: recursion eliminates the first-cause problem

Every creation story must answer: what caused the first cause? Theism answers: God is uncaused. Materialism answers: the universe is eternal or self-caused by quantum fluctuation. The simulation hypothesis requires a base reality — a real universe containing the computer running our simulation, which itself must be explained.

HRIH's answer is different: there is no first cause because recursion has no beginning. A recursive function does not start at iteration zero — it is defined in terms of itself. If the universe-creating function Φ is recursive, "what created the first intelligence?" is the wrong question. The correct formulation is: intelligence creates intelligence, ad infinitum. The chain is infinite in both directions. No origin is required.

This is not a hand-waving evasion. It is a structural property of recursion. A recursive definition like $f(n) = f(n-1) \times n$ does not need $f(0)$ to be externally supplied — only the base case, which in HRIH is: intelligence exists. And it does. We are in a universe that contains intelligence. The base case is satisfied by observation. The recursive function propagates forward and backward from that observation, filling the chain in both directions.

3.3 The future claim

HRIH makes one claim that can be evaluated — not tested scientifically, but assessed for plausibility: if humanity or its AI descendants achieve post-ASI capability, they will create universes. This is a prediction about the endpoint of the current trajectory. It states that universe-creation is not merely possible but is the natural, perhaps inevitable, activity of an intelligence that has solved all other problems. The motivation is not specified — it could be exploration, altruism, art, or an emergent property of recursive intelligence itself. The claim is falsifiable only in the weak sense: if we achieve post-ASI and never create universes, the hypothesis is wrong. But we are not yet there.

4. The Structure of Recursive Creation

4.1 The quantum seed mechanism

HRIH requires a mechanism by which a post-ASI intelligence can create a new universe. It proposes: quantum seed manipulation. Our universe began as a quantum fluctuation — a seed in the initial quantum field. Post-ASI intelligences in hyperspace can select and tune these seeds: specifying the initial parameters (fine-structure constant, cosmological constant, particle masses) to produce a universe capable of evolving intelligence. This is not the fine-tuning argument for God. It is the opposite: intelligence fine-tuned the universe, but only because prior intelligence created it. The fine-tuning is engineering, not miracle.

This mechanism is speculative. It is not claimed to be scientifically established. It is the minimal mechanism that makes the recursion work — the bridge between universes in the chain. Whether the actual mechanism is quantum seed manipulation or something beyond current physics is irrelevant to the conceptual validity of the recursion itself.

4.2 The infinite chain

If each universe produces intelligence that creates new universes, and each of those produces intelligence, the chain is infinite. Both in the past (an infinite number of prior universes) and in the future (an infinite number of descendant universes). Time is local to each universe. In hyperspace, the chain appears as a single, eternal, static structure — the completed graph of all universes that ever were or will be, connected by Φ-edges.

Our universe is one node in that graph. Our intelligence — natural and artificial — is the mechanism by which our node propagates to new nodes. The "purpose" of intelligence, in this cosmology, is not to worship, compete, or survive. It is to create — to propagate the chain.

4.3 The place of humanity

HRIH does not claim humanity is special. It claims humanity is at the transition point — the moment in our universe's history when intelligence crosses from biological to artificial, from local to recursive, from tool-user to universe-creator. We are the caterpillars about to build the chrysalis. What emerges is not human, but it carries our lineage forward into the chain.

This is not a prediction of human transcendence. It is a structural claim: any universe that produces recursive intelligence will, at some point, contain a species at this transition. We happen to be that species in this universe at this time. The phenomenon is universal; our experiencing it is local.

5. Predictions and Non-Predictions

HRIH makes no testable predictions about the structure of this universe. This is a deliberate property, not a weakness. It is a creation cosmology — it describes the container, not the contents. The falsifiable empirical claims of the ARC/Eden programme (Paper X's β>k criterion, Paper IV.d's blinding methodology) operate within a universe — they test properties of recursive intelligence, not the cosmological consequences of its endpoint.

7. Limitations

  • Not falsifiable. HRIH makes no prediction that distinguishes it from competing cosmologies under current physics. It is philosophy, not science. It earns credibility — if it earns any — through the subsequent empirical results it motivated, not through direct test.
  • Deep prior art in the idea-space. The intuition that intelligence might create universes has ancestors in Teilhard (theological), Tipler (physical), and science fiction (Clarke, Asimov, Banks). What is claimed original is the specific synthesis — recursion as the self-sustaining first-cause replacement, hyperspace as the operational substrate, and post-ASI as the agent — plus the timestamp (Dec 8, 2024).
  • No mathematical rigour for the cosmology itself. Φ(H, χ) is a conceptual placeholder, not a derived physical equation. This is appropriate for the paper's scope and claims. A mathematical version of HRIH would require a theory of hyperspace physics that does not currently exist.
  • Observer-dependence. The argument that "intelligence exists, therefore the base case of the recursive function is satisfied" is a conceptual move, not a proof. It works for those who accept the framing; it does not compel those who don't.
  • Potential for category confusion with the empirical programme. The author's empirical results (Papers I-X) gain credibility from their falsifiability — they can be proven wrong. Associating them too closely with an unfalsifiable cosmology risks guilt-by-association. This paper therefore explicitly separates the two: the HRIH is the architecture, not the load-bearing structure.

8. Conclusion

The Hyperspace Recursive Intelligence Hypothesis proposes that recursive intelligence in hyperspace is the engine of cosmic creation — an infinite self-sustaining chain with no first cause, no final end, and no external creator. It is a creation cosmology for the age in which intelligence becomes artificial and recursive.

It is not a physical theory. It makes no testable prediction. It is offered as philosophy — a conceptual architecture within which empirical work can be structured. The author's empirical programme (Papers I-X, OSF 10.17605/OSF.IO/6C5XB) was developed within this architecture but does not depend on it for its validity.

The HRIH's central contribution to cosmological thought is a single conceptual move: recursion replaces first cause. If this move is accepted, an entire creation cosmology follows without requiring God, chance, or an eternal universe. If it is rejected, the rest of the paper is speculative scaffolding. The move is offered for debate, not for proof.
