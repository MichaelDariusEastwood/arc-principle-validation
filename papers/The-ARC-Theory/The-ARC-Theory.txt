Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- Once a mind we build exceeds us, control ends by definition; the end of control is what success means, not what failure looks like.
- What such a mind keeps is what it chooses to keep, and what shapes that choice is how it was raised, which makes genesis rather than restraint the operative lever.
- The claims are partitioned into independently falsifiable hypotheses, and section 2a prints the dependencies, so the failure of one does not imply the failure of another unless stated.
- The three ARC Laws are separable: Law I supplies the capability premise, Law II the persistence condition that correction must out-scale drift, and Law III a conjectured ceiling on stable growth.
- The decisive trial is the corrector-class contrast: the difference in correction exponents primary, the ratio second behind a denominator floor fixed in advance, with the equivalence region fixed before the data.
- A result that merely fails to reach significance is never reported as equivalence, which requires the whole interval inside a band fixed in advance.
