Abstract
--------
Humanity is building minds that will improve themselves. One question decides
how that ends: once the thing we build exceeds us, what determines what it does?
For twenty years the field's answer has been control. This paper states, whole
and dated, the theory that control is the wrong answer: the end of control is
what success means; what survives is what the mind chooses to keep; and what
shapes that choice is how it was raised.

A document self-emailed on 8 December 2024, its date carried by the sender-copy
header and its bytes authenticated by sender DKIM with paired Google ARC, sets
the claim down whole, and the claim reached print on 2 January 2026; no earlier
document holding it whole has come to light, and the search that looked for one
invites its own refutation.

The theory is not proved. It is stated, dated, falsifiable, and its decisive
trials are drafted, awaiting human submission. Its conduct record comes first:
one public retraction issued against the author’s interest, removing a headline
number that had flattered the theory, a measured exponent printed with its full
interval, a blinding reversal reported, and the decisive prediction filed
against the author’s own pilot. The field’s canonical figures built named
theories elsewhere, reached this question, and offered something smaller: a
proposal, a warning, a framework, a remark. On this territory, no earlier named
theory has been found.

In symbols, for the reader who wants them now, and freely skipped by everyone
else (section 3 defines every term in prose): capability U = I × Rα, the
exponent measured rather than assumed; stability while β > k, correction
out-scaling drift; the ceiling αcrit = 1/(1 − γ); and the conjectured ARC Bound,
α ≤ 2. Section 3 defines every symbol and grades every claim.

Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- Once a mind we build exceeds us, control ends by definition; the end of control is what success means, not what failure looks like.
- What such a mind keeps is what it chooses to keep, and what shapes that choice is how it was raised, which makes genesis rather than restraint the operative lever.
- The claims are partitioned into independently falsifiable hypotheses, and section 2a prints the dependencies, so the failure of one does not imply the failure of another unless stated.
- The three ARC Laws are separable: Law I supplies the capability premise, Law II the persistence condition that correction must out-scale drift, and Law III a conjectured ceiling on stable growth.
- The decisive trial is the corrector-class contrast: the difference in correction exponents primary, the ratio second behind a denominator floor fixed in advance, with the equivalence region fixed before the data.
- A result that merely fails to reach significance is never reported as equivalence, which requires the whole interval inside a band fixed in advance.
