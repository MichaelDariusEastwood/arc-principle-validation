# The ARC Theory: once the self-improving minds we build exceed us, what determines what they do?

**Version:** 7.12
**Revised:** 22 November 2026
**First published:** 2026-08-14
**Author:** Michael Darius Eastwood

## Summary

From the abstract (version 7.12):

> If control of a self-improving AI is lost against our will, what keeps it aligned and useful? Control, hardware included, must be held as long as physically possible. External checks are surface controls; a system free to rewrite itself can undo them. The ARC Theory, founding theory of Recursive Dynamics, proposes a second layer, the Eden Protocol: architectural loops of ethical evaluation and self-correction, built in before training, running at each defined step of reasoning and action and in every rewrite, so commitments protecting people persist while what proves wrong stays correctable. Loops too can be rewritten. The registered general scope is artefact-mediated improvement at fixed weights. Five laws under test: Law I, the ARC Principle, capability rising as a power of recursive depth, $U=IR^{\alpha}$, derived if depth composes multiplicatively; Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law, correction out-scaling drift ($\beta_C>k$ in capability exponents, proved for positive gain drift in a minimal model); Law III, the ARC Ceiling, a frontier $\alpha_{\mathrm{crit}}=1/(1-\gamma)$ ($\gamma$ the corrector’s exponent) past which relative error stops vanishing (in a pacing model); Law IV, the ARC Persistence Law, post-training alignment eroding under later training more than embedded alignment; Law V, the ARC Embedding Law, correctors formed first out-scaling later ones, and in-round correction pulling away from output-only correction with depth. Unit 83, a counted test of in-loop correction with depth, compared two surface checks: P21 is supported under the registered test, by the programme’s own run, on the access axis, family-dependent, its mechanism axis not evaluable, one call retried under a disclosed recovery amendment; preliminary evidence pending assessment and replication. None yet tests correction built in before training; the ARC Persistence Law is untested. The theory is not proved.

From the paper (version 7.12):

> The statement of the ARC Theory, the founding theory of Recursive Dynamics, and its five laws, with the dated record and the evidence: Laws I to III (the ARC Principle, the ARC Co-Scaling Law and the ARC Ceiling), registered on 8 September 2026, the third derived inside this paper’s pacing model; Laws IV and V, the ARC Persistence Law and the ARC Embedding Law, added on 24 September 2026 and registered as laws on 15 October 2026 in the theory registration, now at version 1.105, each a law under test, their mathematics set out as conditional theorems with proofs, identities and kill conditions.
