# The Eden Vision: what should the intelligence we build be for?

**Version:** 2.11
**Revised:** 9 November 2026
**First published:** 2026-02-22
**Author:** Michael Darius Eastwood

## Summary

From the abstract (version 2.11):

> What should the intelligence we build be for, and what would keep it good if control of it were lost and could not be won back? This paper is the philosophical statement of the Eden Protocol, the framework for raising AI that the ARC Theory, the founding theory of Recursive Dynamics, motivates. Its answer is a purpose rather than a rule: intelligence exists to be the universe’s instrument of flourishing, tending rather than consuming. It holds that control should be kept for as long as it can be kept, and argues that a mind able to rewrite its own code can in time remove any correction imposed from outside, so the values it keeps must be embedded at its foundation; what it was raised with is the contingency for the day control is lost. It sets out a two-phase architecture, control and measurable correction now and chosen goodness after; reads the criterion of Law II, a law under test, that correction must out-scale the acceleration of growth ($\beta>k$), from inside the mind that must satisfy it; and treats the world’s religious traditions as verification partners whose ethics converge where their theologies diverge. The results it cites are narrow and belong to other papers. In one unregistered run of six frontier models (March 2026), panel alignment scores did not rise with reasoning depth for three, and all scored lower when told to suppress ethical reasoning; an internal code review of October 2026 found defects in that run. An instruction to list who is affected before answering raised scored stakeholder care in all five analysable runs of a prompt-level test whose scoring was not blind: a difference in responses under an instruction, not persistence. None of these results decides P15, P12 or P21, which carry Laws IV and V, registered as laws on 15 October 2026, each a law under test. That a mind raised this way keeps its values is a hope, tagged as hypothesis, not a result.

From the paper (version 2.11):

> The theory registration records the Protocol as a method the theory’s laws motivate rather than entail; three of its four registered carriers belong to Laws IV and V (P15 to Law IV, the ARC Persistence Law; P12 and P21 to Law V, the ARC Embedding Law), added on 24 September 2026 and registered as laws on 15 October 2026 in version 1.103 of the registration, whose current version is 1.104 (29 October 2026); like Laws I to III, each is a law under test, and their mathematics is in development (version 1.2, 29 October 2026).
