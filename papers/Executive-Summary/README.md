## Summary

From the abstract (version 3.30):

> How can a self-improving AI stay useful and aligned if its controls are one day lost? Control, hardware included, is held for as long as it physically can be and never given up by choice. Beneath it the programme proposes the Eden Protocol: loops of ethical evaluation and self-correction built into the architecture before training and run at each defined step of reasoning and action and in every rewrite. The ARC Theory, the founding theory of Recursive Dynamics, states five laws under test. Law I, the ARC Principle, is the foundation: capability grows as a power of recursive depth, $U=IR^{\alpha}$; its first form, $U=IR$, dates from 8 December 2024. Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law: correction stays ahead of drift where its exponent exceeds drift’s, $\beta_C>k$. Law III, the ARC Ceiling: in a pacing model, correction falls behind past a frontier, $\alpha_{\mathrm{crit}}=1/(1-\gamma)$, with $\gamma$ the corrector’s exponent. Law IV, the ARC Persistence Law: alignment added after training erodes under later training more than alignment embedded in it. Law V, the ARC Embedding Law: a corrector formed first out-scales one added later, and correction in each revision round gains on output-only correction with depth. In Unit 83, a counted test of in-loop correction with depth, P21 is supported under the registered test, by the programme’s own run, on the access axis. The result is family-dependent, its mechanism axis is not evaluable, and one call was retried under a disclosed recovery amendment. It compared two checks on trained systems; nothing yet tests correction built in before training, or persistence. Earlier alignment studies are single-run evidence; error-decay fits identify no capability-growth exponent. The Eden Protocol’s software is a partial implementation; the HARI Treaty, the Draft Treaty on Hardware-Aligned Recursive Intelligence, is a consultation proposal. No law is proved.

From The ARC Theory (version 7.12), on the status of Laws IV and V:

> Laws IV and V (the ARC Persistence Law and the ARC Embedding Law) were added on 24 September 2026 and registered as laws on 15 October 2026 in version 1.103, each a law under test; their mathematics is set out in their own report, at version 1.4 of 22 November 2026, as conditional theorems with proofs, identities and kill conditions.
