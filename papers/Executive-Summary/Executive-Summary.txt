Abstract
--------
As AI gets smarter, it does not reliably get safer, and the methods used to
measure safety are themselves unreliable. This executive summary is the map of
the whole programme: thirty-one documents in the public register, twenty-nine of
them papers by the site's own counting rule, the ARC Theory's five laws carried
as named conjectures (Laws IV and V added on 24 September 2026, their
propositions registered, neither law yet registered as a law or tested, their
mathematics set out at version 1.0 of 3 October 2026), the Eden Protocol
intervention, the field of Recursive Dynamics that the author founded, the
theory-level registration that fixed twenty-two severable propositions before
any of them could be scored, and a study estate whose deciding instruments are
written and dated, their schedules regenerable by any reader from a published
seed: one unit, the corrector-class audit, was registered on OSF on 1 October
2026 and became public on 4 October 2026, at the end of OSF's approval period
(DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/RV2B5), and every other is a draft awaiting the author's
submission, preregistered by timestamp where a receipt exists. Every claim is

tests are written down before they are run.

Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- As AI gets smarter, it does not reliably get safer: under four-layer blinding, alignment scaling splits into three architecture-dependent tiers, from d = +1.38 (improvement with deeper thinking) to d = -0.53 (degradation), and for most architectures tested alignment does not scale at all.
- The measurement problem is as serious as the alignment problem: introducing clinical-trial-grade blinding changed one model's association from significantly positive to significantly negative and moved another to null, which is a reason to distrust unblinded AI-on-AI evaluation.
- One intervention result survives across architectures: asking a model to list the people its answer affects (stakeholder care) improved reasoning quality in all five analysable model runs; the earlier Fisher-combined significance figure is withdrawn, and the per-model results stand on their own.
- The framework carries five laws as named conjectures, never as established results: the ARC Principle (capability compounds with recursive depth), the ARC Co-Scaling Law (stability requires correction to out-scale drift), the ARC Ceiling (the stability ceiling is the reciprocal of the corrector's shortfall), with the ARC Bound of two as the Ceiling's value at one half, and the ARC Persistence Law and the ARC Embedding Law, added on 24 September 2026, their propositions registered, neither law yet registered as a law or tested, their mathematics set out at version 1.0 of 3 October 2026; the correction leverage gamma has never been measured, by anyone.
- The questions now have their own name: Recursive Dynamics, a new field at the Carnot stage, with five state variables conjectured to be substrate-independent, fifty-one printed objections with dispositions, and a founding paper whose name carries its own kill condition (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/HCPBU).
- Every deciding experiment is drafted, dated and frozen before it is run: one unit, the corrector-class audit (unit 06), registered on OSF on 1 October 2026 and public since 4 October 2026, and every other a draft awaiting the author's submission; schedules regenerable byte-for-byte from a published seed; and an adversarially verified coverage matrix mapping forty-two claims to the tests that would decide them.
