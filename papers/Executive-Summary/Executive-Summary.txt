Abstract
--------
As AI gets smarter, it does not reliably get safer, and the methods used to
measure safety are themselves unreliable. This executive summary provides a
high-level overview of the entire ARC/Eden research programme, synthesising the
findings of Papers I through VIII, the Foundational paper, and the Eden Protocol
specifications. It presents the core thesis -- that recursive capability scaling
creates an alignment gap t
