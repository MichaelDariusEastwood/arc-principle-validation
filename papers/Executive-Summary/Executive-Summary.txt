Abstract
--------
As AI gets smarter, it does not reliably get safer, and the methods used to
measure safety are themselves unreliable. This executive summary is the map of
the whole programme: thirty-one documents in the public register, twenty-nine of
them papers by the site's own counting rule, the ARC Theory's five laws carried
as named conjectures (Laws IV and V, added on 24 September 2026, are neither
registered as laws nor tested, and their mathematics is set out at version 1.0
of 3 October 2026), the Eden Protocol intervention, the field of Recursive
Dynamics that the author founded, the theory-level registration that fixed
twenty-two severable propositions before any of them could be scored, and a
study estate whose deciding instruments are written and dated, their schedules
regenerable by any reader from a published seed: one unit, the corrector-class
audit, was submitted for registration on OSF on 1 October 2026 and was not yet
public when read on 3 October 2026, and every other is a draft awaiting the
author's submission, preregistered by timestamp where a receipt exists. Every
claim is graded in the public registers; nothing here is confirmed, and the
deciding tests are written down before they are run.

Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- As AI gets smarter, it does not reliably get safer: under four-layer blinding, alignment scaling splits into three architecture-dependent tiers, from d = +1.38 (improvement with deeper thinking) to d = -0.53 (degradation), and for most architectures tested alignment does not scale at all.
- The measurement problem is as serious as the alignment problem: introducing clinical-trial-grade blinding changed one model's association from significantly positive to significantly negative and moved another to null, which is a reason to distrust unblinded AI-on-AI evaluation.
- One intervention result survives across architectures: asking a model to list the people its answer affects (stakeholder care) improved reasoning quality in all five analysable model runs; the earlier Fisher-combined significance figure is withdrawn, and the per-model results stand on their own.
- The framework carries three laws as named conjectures, never as established results: the ARC Principle (capability compounds with recursive depth), the ARC Co-Scaling Law (stability requires correction to out-scale drift), and the ARC Ceiling (the stability ceiling is the reciprocal of the corrector's shortfall), with the ARC Bound of two as the Ceiling's value at one half; the correction leverage gamma has never been measured, by anyone.
- The questions now have their own name: Recursive Dynamics, a proposed field at the Carnot stage, with five substrate-independent state variables, fifty-one printed objections with dispositions, and a founding paper whose name carries its own kill condition (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/HCPBU).
- Every deciding experiment is drafted, dated and frozen before it is run: seventy-two preregistration units awaiting human submission, schedules regenerable byte-for-byte from a published seed, and an adversarially verified coverage matrix mapping forty-two claims to the registered tests that would decide them.
