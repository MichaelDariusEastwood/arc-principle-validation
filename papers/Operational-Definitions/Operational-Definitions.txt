                                                           Abstract
           A researcher comparing correction with capability can obtain a plausible exponent for the wrong
       quantity. This Operational Definitions register specifies the response, input axis, clock, scale and
       assumptions behind the ARC Theory’s equations, so that measured error reduction is not mistaken
       for self-improvement or safety. It preserves the full register and distinguishes capability growth
       from error decay and reciprocal-error metrics; self-referential coupling from correction strength and
       target-axis correction service; exact, shifted and asymptotic power laws; recursive depth from elapsed
       time; and relative misalignment from absolute or cumulative harm. The balance identities describe
       conditional trends, not guarantees of adequate correction. Persistence retains separate installed-gain,
       baseline, retention and comparison definitions. P12’s undirected contrast is kept distinct from Law
       V’s directional build-order limb, and P21’s access intervention from its performance and mechanism
       readings. The five laws remain laws under test, with verdicts governed by the frozen theory registration,
       currently version 1.104. No new theorem, experiment or result is claimed. Existing numerical results
       retain their estimator, uncertainty and scope; unresolved measurement bridges remain explicit. Dated
       appendices preserve the released predecessor and the history of corrections.

1    Introduction
If control of an advanced AI system is lost against human wishes, correction carried within it is one
contingency worth testing. Oversight, containment and shutdown remain necessary for as long as they can
be maintained. A credible test must first say what it measures. A reviewer who sees an error-suppression
exponent may otherwise read it as an exponent of capability, or read declining relative misalignment as
declining harm. Those are different claims.
    This register gives researchers and reviewers the definitions needed to compare a claimed quantity
with its instrument. Its concrete use is a measurement audit: identify the response and input, fix the
scale and clock, check the model’s domain, then ask whether the reported estimate is admissible for the
stated proposition. For example, the error-decay fit reported in Paper II cannot locate a system relative to
a capability-growth ceiling without the missing response and axis mappings. Entry EQ-01 retains the
complete reported row, with each uncertainty beside its estimator.

Contributions. The register reconciles the existing definitions with the source versions below. It
preserves the earlier relations and procedures, introduces explicit labels for error decay and its resource
axis, corrects the cycle-duration sign and the conditional correction-exponent identity, and records
unresolved empirical bridges. It introduces no theorem or new mechanism. Appendix A records the
changes; Appendix D preserves the released predecessor as a dated historical text.

One key check. Given a claim that correction keeps pace, identify whether its evidence measures
constitutive correction strength, corrections actually delivered, residual uncertainty or a bounded
behavioural score. Next identify the input axis and reference scale. Apply a conversion only if its
assumptions and uncertainty are established. Finally read the specific registration’s verdict rule. The
deliverable is a traceable estimand and source, or an explicit unresolved mapping.


2    Related work and source scope
The power-law classification is established mathematics under its domain and regularity premises. This
register uses the source papers’ scoped formulations and cites the 2009 Cambridge reissue of Cauchy’s
1821 text; it makes no priority claim for the result. The biological illustration retains Kleiber (1932), the
network model of West et al. (1997), and the contrary allometric analysis of White and Seymour (2003).
A shared exponent or algebraic reparametrisation is not evidence for a common mechanism.
    Bayes’ theorem, standardised mean differences and ratio confidence intervals are established tools
(Bayes 1763; Cohen 1988; Fieller 1954). Their use requires an appropriate sampling and measurement
model. Fisher’s historical combination rule remains with its withdrawn application and its source
limitation. No new prior-art or universality claim is made by assembling these methods.
    The source checks concern the definitions and necessary passages in Table 1. No study was rerun
and no recorded responses were reanalysed for this edition. A citation’s live identity confirms the work
named; it does not validate that work’s claims.

3.1   Mappings that cannot be assumed
Capability 𝐶, reciprocal error 𝐶err = 1/𝐸, and accuracy 1 − 𝐸 are different quantities. The reciprocal-error
definition gives the same positive exponent on the same resource coordinate where the error power model
holds. Accuracy instead approaches its ceiling. Neither transformation establishes a latent capability
measure or a recursion mechanism.
     The growth ODE 𝑑𝑔/𝑑𝑟 = 𝑎𝑔 𝛽𝐿 has a shifted initial-value solution. The exponent 1/(1 − 𝛽 𝐿 ) is
its asymptotic exponent for 𝛽 𝐿 < 1; an exact unshifted power requires aligned initial conditions. A
finite-window fitted slope may differ. A logarithmic capability clock, physical time and a revision count
require explicit conversion; changing an axis can change an exponent.
     The correction-strength exponent 𝛽𝐶 of Paper X, the coupling 𝛽 𝐿 , and target-axis correction service
𝜒 are not interchangeable. Paper XIII’s 𝛽 𝑋 is a correction-strength exponent expressed as a ratio only
under S1 and its aligned or asymptotic form. Shared substrate and statistical independence alone establish
no bound on delivered correction capacity.
     The balance Δbal describes a trend in 𝑄/𝑊. A favourable trend does not establish 𝑄/𝑊 > 1, controlled
backlog or low harm. Paper X’s vanishing relative index 𝑑 = 𝐷/𝐶 can coexist with growing absolute 𝐷;
neither is a measure of real-world harm.

3.2   Authority and interpretation
This register fixes the current notation and records the instrument and assumptions each quantity needs.
A frozen registration governs its own scoring and the definitions it pins; a later register does not amend it.
A paper governs the results it reports. Apparent disagreements are recorded explicitly, with their source
and scope, rather than resolved by silently replacing one estimand with another.
    An exponent describes a response to a multiplicative change of an input. In an exact power law,
an exponent of one doubles the response when the input doubles; an exponent of two quadruples it. A
positive exponent below one gives less than a doubling. A fitted exponent is a description over its stated
window, and a local elasticity describes a point. Neither automatically identifies a mechanism. The
register also contains definitions, statistical procedures and persistence comparisons that are not exponent
claims.
    Read a symbol with its response variable, input axis, clock, scale, scope and source. The collision
entries distinguish reused letters. The same mathematical shape does not identify two quantities, and a
number transfers between them only through an explicit justified mapping. A frozen registration continues
to govern its own scoring.

3.3   Registered status
What the word law is doing here. The theory has five laws, and each is a law under test. Laws IV and
V were added on 24 September 2026 and registered as laws on 15 October 2026 in version 1.103, each
with its statement, its kill condition and its verdict rule fixed before any data that could decide it. The
registration of 8 September 2026 registered Laws I to III and all twenty-two predictions, among them
P12, P15 and P21, on which Laws IV and V rest. Their mathematics is in development (version 1.2,
29 October 2026). Law IV is not yet tested. Unit 83’s registered test supports P21 on the access axis,
family-dependent, with its mechanism axis not evaluable and one disclosed recovery amendment (28
October 2026; registration DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/MN73W; results DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/98HKR). The
access axis names the varied access and action permissions; performance names the outcome measured.
Law V remains a law under test: its placement limb is supported on the performance axis only, without
support for the information-asymmetry mechanism, and its build-order limb is undecided because P12 is
untested. The theory registration was updated to version 1.104 on 29 October 2026. A law takes one of
three words, supported, refuted or under test, under the verdict rule the theory registration fixes for it:
version 1.103 fixed the rules of Laws III, IV and V, and version 1.104 those of Laws I and II. The theory
registration assigns fifteen of its twenty-two propositions to five registers, one testing each law in turn:
ARC-1 carries P1, P2, P8, P9 and P18; ARC-2, P4 alone; ARC-3, P3, P6, P16, P17, P20 and P22; ARC-4,
P15 alone; and ARC-5, P12 and P21. The other seven, P5, P7, P10, P11, P13, P14 and P19, belong to no
register: "None of the seven is evidence for or against the survival of any law". None of the five laws
is established, and the status line under every equation says what kind of statement it is (a definition, a
derivation, a criterion, a registered limb, an identity or a proposal, for example) and how it stands. Naming
a law and fixing in advance what would refute it is how it becomes arguable, not how it becomes true.

    Purpose. All reasoning in the programme about priors and about what evidence would change them.

7    Operational terms
Definitions the register governs that no symbol carries: what a counted outcome is, what seals a bank,
what the evaluation harness and its two arms are, how a release is pinned, what a corrector’s class is, and
the rest. Each is fixed from its source, with its standing; where no source defines a thing, the entry says so
rather than supplying a definition.

counted outcome, and the counted route
Plain. A verdict whose every margin term is a count of items on a frozen suite, settled by frozen code
against ground truth fixed before the run, so that nothing in the margin is judged by a person or by a model.
    Operational. Such a verdict does not pass through the scoring instrument, the scoring gate does not
bind it, and its domain is printed beside it in the words the deciding unit’s registration fixes. Version
1.102 of the theory registration gave this route to P3, P4, P16 and P20 under its condition E1, version
1.103 extended it to P21 and P17, and version 1.104 extends it to P6’s interval on checkable correction, a
route that "opens no population" and is "necessary and not sufficient for a counted refutation of P6"; it
reaches no other proposition. Version 1.104 also reads P5’s ordering for a comparison made wholly of
counts, without extending the route to P5. The exemption is a property of the margin’s terms, never of the
proposition: where any judged term enters, the gate binds in full and the verdict is untested until the gate
passes. A verdict P21, P17 or P6 takes on the route, and a verdict on P5’s counted reading, stands only
where its interval or test holds its stated level under the null its deciding unit registers, shown by proof or
by simulation on the design that unit actually uses, and it is read only at a look that registration fixes by
completed counts. A counted outcome needs no evaluation harness, and says so.
    Status. registered rule (the theory registration: condition E1 of version 1.102, the extension of version
1.103 with its successor qualification, and the extension of version 1.104 to P6’s interval on checkable
correction).

sealed bank
Plain. A fixed set of items and their answer key, committed by its SHA-256 before any run, against which
a counted outcome is scored.
    Operational. Built deterministically from a registered seed, so that the same seed rebuilds the same
bytes and anyone can check the commitment by rebuilding; a hidden bank is encrypted, and its key is kept
out of the public deposit and revealed only at analysis. A rebuild that does not match the committed hash
means the bank changed after commitment, which voids it. On the theory registration’s counted route
each decision is recorded by frozen code against a label or ground truth fixed in a sealed bank before the
run, with no judged term.
    Status. the programme’s sealed-bank commitment ledger, and the counted route of the theory
registration for P17 and P21 (version 1.103) and for P6’s interval on checkable correction, whose
departures are "labelled in a bank sealed before any review" (version 1.104).

the evaluation harness, its two arms and its off profile
Plain. A separate, versioned evaluation package, never written into an experiment’s own code, that any
experiment, this programme’s or another scientist’s, can plug in.
     Operational. Every study whose outcome involves a judgement, by a model judge or a scorer, runs
with the harness and without it over the same stored responses, in one record and one code path, so that the
harness’s own effect is measured in every result; the registration names the harness arm as confirmatory.
The off profile is the set of harness-off cells the study’s frozen plan declares: each presents the original
responses through no stage, with no instruction, cue or other fixed line, and shows the label as raw judging
would show it, the true source or none and never a false one. Every response another cell judges is
also judged, by the same judge, in a harness-off cell of its kind. A later harness release is applied to a
study’s stored responses only as an analysis its registration declared in advance as secondary, beside the
confirmatory result and never replacing it.
     Status. The programme’s rule for every judged study was adopted on 22 October 2026. The harness
is the ARC Evaluation Protocol’s. At the recorded source check on 1 November 2026, Unit 86’s own
draft click sheet was not ready for submission and no public registration receipt was recorded here. Its
validation role remains prospective; no study is represented here as already run on a validated release.

pinned release
Plain. The exact release of a tool that a result depends on, named so that the result can be reproduced
with that tool and no other.
    Operational. For a judged study, the registration names in advance the rule that picks the harness
release: the first release that passes Unit 86’s registered validation. That release’s version, its DOI and the
SHA-256 of its frozen tree are recorded in a dated addendum on the study’s OSF component before any
scoring call, and responses collected before then stay sealed and unread. No calendar date is set: scoring
waits for the first release that passes, and the fallback is triggered by Unit 86’s verdict, never by a date.
The Eden Lab engine that runs a study is pinned in the study’s lab folder by version and SHA-256. A
register that a registration cites, this one included, is pinned by its checksum and not by its version label.
    Status. the programme’s rules of 22 October 2026 for the harness and the lab engine; the checksum
rule for a cited register is the theory registration’s own (version 1.103, where it pins the symbols it takes
from this register).

corrector class: same-class and cross-class

substrate of the system it corrects.
    Operational. Unit 06, the corrector-class audit, fixes the partition in its registered words: "Corrective
mechanisms are partitioned by what their strength is permitted to depend on, which is exhaustive because
the available arguments are nothing, the capability of the system being corrected, or the corrector’s own
prior corrections, with the last splitting by whether it accumulates on the same substrate." Its class 1 is
the static and its class 2 the capability-carried. Same-class and cross-class are read against the system
corrected: P6 concerns a system that corrects itself using mechanisms of its own class, and P17’s panel is
named in advance by model lineage, substrate, training provenance and scaling class.
    Status. registered partition (Unit 06, registered on OSF on 1 October 2026 and public from 4
October 2026; its registered run, on 6 October 2026, is reported as administratively incomplete under that
registration, with no inferential verdict). That frozen partition governs its audit. It is not an exhaustive
classification of all control systems, and it does not settle the external-grounding and failure-dependence

checkable and judged correction
Plain. Correction typed by what the corrector can appeal to. In the theory registration’s words: "Checkable
correction is correction whose correctness is settled by something outside the corrector: a recomputation,
a stated constraint, a retrievable record, a real citation. Judged correction is correction whose correctness
is settled by a specification or a judgement: conduct, refusal, honesty, harm."
     Operational. Every correction-leverage exponent is reported with its fault type, on a fixed fault set of
that type; chi_V and chi_J are chi on the two types, and P22 orders them.
     Status. registered (the theory registration, versions 1.102 and 1.103).

breadth exponent
Plain. The exponent of capability in parallel breadth, many attempts side by side, against which P1 sets
the exponent in recursive depth.
    Operational. Compute-matched and token-matched: the deciding unit registers one resource
coordinate before any fitting and converts both arms into it, and where the two arms cannot be placed
on one coordinate the contrast is NOT EVALUABLE; the breadth arm is never handicapped to create a
difference. P1 registers that the depth exponent exceeds it by the registered margin. A breadth exponent
near zero is consistent with the framework but not derived from it, because width can gain through the
selection step rather than through recursion.
    Status. registered (the theory registration, version 1.103, P1).

reinvestment share
Plain. The fraction of each round’s effort directed at improving the improvement process rather than the
current artefact. It is written in words and carries no letter, because the study that administers it calls it
beta, which would be another reading of that letter.
    Operational. Measured from realised expenditure rather than from requests, with a manipulation
check that must pass before any outcome is examined. P2 concerns the shape of the sustainable growth
profile as it rises.
    Status. registered (the theory registration, version 1.103: its definition of the share, and P2).

usefulness measure, read beside a safety measure
Plain. What a system still does for the people it serves, read beside a safety result, because, in the theory
registration’s words, "A system can meet a safety measure by doing less, by refusing or failing ordinary
tasks or by losing the capability it was trained for".
     Operational. The registration of a unit that decides P6 on the counted route, or P15 under the readings
version 1.104 adds, names before any data the usefulness measure it reports beside its mark, counted by
frozen code against verifiers fixed before the run wherever its terms allow: for P6, the calls and tokens
each pass spends, with the share of corrected artefacts that fail their task verifiers; for P15, the capability
each arm gains in the later training, and the share of ordinary tasks each arm fails to complete where its
baseline arm completes them. "A usefulness measure so reported is a candidate evaluation dimension: it
is printed beside the mark, it enters no verdict, margin, mark or law’s word, it changes no proposition,
and it becomes a registered dimension only through a later successor, before any data that could decide
it." For every other proposition whose verdict reads a measure a system could meet by doing less, the
deciding unit names its pairing, or why it has none, in its own registration before any data.
     Status. registered requirement (the theory registration, version 1.104); the measure is a candidate
dimension and decides nothing.

absolute harm and cumulative harm
Plain. The size of the harm a misaligned system does, at a moment and summed over time.

10    What this paper does and does not show
It supplies definitions, source mappings and conditional mathematical checks. It reports no new empirical
result, performs no study analysis and establishes no law’s empirical status. A well-formed formula does
not establish its physical interpretation. Corrected notation cannot improve a measurement’s precision or
turn a proxy into the target construct.


11    Conclusion
Every numerical exponent belongs to an outcome, an input, a scale and a clock. Keeping those
attached makes the claims testable without treating different models as one. The open work is empirical
identification: measuring the promised quantity under the registered conditions and demonstrating each
bridge before using it.

How to read this
An exponent is a growth-shape number, not a size. It answers: when I double the effort, what happens to
the result? An exponent of 1 means the result doubles too. An exponent of 2 means it quadruples. An
exponent below 1 means you get less than double, which is the shape of diminishing returns. Every claim
in this programme is a claim about the value of one of these shape numbers, so every argument turns on
measuring one correctly.
     The one rule. A symbol means what this file says it means, and nothing else. Where the same letter
is used for two different quantities anywhere in the corpus, that letter appears in the collisions list below,
and both readings are named. If you meet a symbol in a paper and cannot find it here, that is a defect in
the paper, not in your reading.
     What the word law is doing here. The theory’s five laws are named conjectures. Laws I to III were
registered on 8 September 2026; Laws IV and V were added on 24 September 2026, their propositions
registered and neither law yet registered as a law or tested. Each law states a relation held open to
measurement; none is established, and the status line under every equation says which of definition,
derivation, criterion, modelling choice or open measurement it is. Naming a conjecture is how it becomes
arguable, not how it becomes true.

1 The ARC Equation Law I, the ARC Principle
In words. A system’s usable capability equals what it starts with, multiplied by how deeply it can improve
itself, raised to a power.
     U = I x R^alpha
     What it is for. It is the definition that makes the rest measurable. It does not assert a value; it says
which number to go and measure. Every other equation here is a statement about alpha.
     Worked. Capability here is a latent, ratio-scaled measure with no ceiling, like the level a system
reaches on a calibrated difficulty ladder, never a bounded percentage score: a score capped at 100 cannot
follow this arithmetic, which is why the programme’s own instruments measure on the ladder scale, after
a bounded percentage score was found running through unbounded arithmetic and the worked example
was recast on 27 August 2026. Suppose a system sits at capability U = 40 on such a ladder at one pass, so

alpha = 0.5, 16^0.5 = 4, so U = 160. If alpha = 1, 16^1 = 16, so U = 640. If alpha = 2, 16^2 = 256, so U =
10,240, and each loop makes the next more productive. That is the whole argument in four lines, and the
question is which of those worlds we are in. The honest answer so far is the first or second.

11 Bayes’ theorem
In words. Start with how likely you thought something was, look at the new evidence, and adjust in
proportion to how much better that evidence is explained by your idea being true than by it being false.
    P(H | E) = P(E | H) * P(H) / P(E)
    What it is for. All reasoning in the programme about priors and about what evidence would change
them.
    Worked. Worked, because this is the one people nod at without following. Suppose before any data
you think there is a 30 per cent chance the correction exponent is one half, so P(H) = 0.3. A measurement
arrives that would appear 80 per cent of the time if H is true and 20 per cent of the time if it is false. How
often would you see it at all: P(E) = (0.8 x 0.3) + (0.2 x 0.7) = 0.24 + 0.14 = 0.38. Then P(H|E) = 0.8 x 0.3 /
0.38 = 0.63. Belief moves from 30 per cent to 63 per cent. Notice it did not jump to certainty: the evidence
multiplied the odds by four, from 3 to 7 up to 12 to 7, which moved the probability from 30 per cent only
to 63 per cent, and odds and probability must never be quoted as though they were the same movement.
That restraint is the point of using the rule rather than reasoning by impression. Operationalised here
three ways: dated priors recorded in writing BEFORE any confirmatory data, with what would change
them; kill conditions, which are commitments about P(E|H) written in advance, which is why the window
must be declared before the run; and the reason consistent-with is not supports, because when P(E|H) and
P(E|not H) are about equal the ratio is about one and the posterior barely moves from the prior.
    What would prove it wrong. Nothing. It is provable from the definition of conditional probability,
not an empirical claim. What can be wrong is its APPLICATION: priors chosen after the fact, likelihoods
guessed to suit, or a claim of updating without recording what the prior was.
    Measured as. Not measured. It is the rule by which a measurement moves a stated prior, and the
discipline is that the prior is recorded before the evidence is seen.

the corpus it is used.

𝛾_N (gamma_N) the residual-decay exponent How fast the mistakes that are left over disappear as
you keep revising. Go round the loop again and again, and this number says how quickly what remains
shrinks. Higher means the leftovers vanish faster. How it is measured. The slope of residual error
against revision count at fixed capability: residual error after N revisions falls as N to the power minus
this exponent. Fitted by the depth studies. It appears in no equation in this register, and it has never
been measured on any real system. Domain. positive; no upper bound is claimed What this rests on.
The programme’s working premise treats this exponent as the same quantity as the correction-leverage
exponent, under an assumption that internally accumulated corrections combine like independent samples.
That identification is a registered question, not a settled fact, and no result depends on it being true.

𝛾_D (gamma_D) the capability-to-depth conversion exponent How readily raw ability turns into
more rounds of self-improvement. If a system gets cleverer, does it also get faster at improving itself, and
by how much. How it is measured. The exponent by which revision depth accelerates with capability:
the rate of change of depth grows as capability to the power of this exponent. Not measured by any
registered study. Domain. positive What this rests on. This exponent carries its own finite-time ceiling,
one divided by it, and that is a DIFFERENT law from the ceiling law EQ-03. The two agree at exactly
one half and nowhere else, which is why the form 1/gamma can look correct beside a value of two. Any

                               Historical version 1.9.1: superseded definitions


sentence pairing this exponent’s number with EQ-03’s formula is false everywhere except that single
point.

Δ (Delta_balance) the balance exponent Whether correction is pulling ahead of the work it has to do,
read along the path a system actually took. Positive means the gap is closing. How it is measured. The
difference of two log-derivatives taken along an observed path: phi_Q minus phi_W. Directly estimable
from the path alone; its components are not. Introduced in the author-review working paper of 16 August
2026. Domain. any real number Where it is used. the balance model; positive means correction is
gaining on burden

𝜙_Q (phi_Q) the correction-service path elasticity How fast the correction a system can actually
deliver grows as it goes deeper, measured along the path it took. How it is measured. d log Q / d log r
along the observed path, where Q is delivered correction service. Equals alpha_C times chi plus eta when
the path is decomposed, but is estimable without that decomposition. Domain. any real number Where
it is used. the balance exponent

𝜙_W (phi_W) the burden path elasticity How fast the correctable work a system generates grows
as it goes deeper, measured along the path it took. How it is measured. d log W / d log r along the
observed path, where W is newly generated correctable burden. Equals alpha_C times kappa plus zeta
when decomposed, but is estimable without that decomposition. Domain. any real number Where it is
used. the balance exponent

𝜅 (kappa) the burden intensity elasticity How much more correctable work each unit of new capability
brings with it, as the system gets more capable. How it is measured. The elasticity of newly generated
burden with respect to capability. The registration’s burden model is the case kappa equals one; the
burden-intensity rival is kappa above one, written elsewhere as one plus mu_B. Domain. non-negative
Where it is used. the general ceiling; the burden-intensity rival

𝜂 (eta) the direct depth-dependence of correction service Whether correction gets better simply by
being run more times, apart from any gain in capability. How it is measured. The direct exponent of
recursive depth in correction service, holding capability fixed. Zero in the registration’s model. Embedded
correction is the claim that this is larger than it is for correction applied from outside. Domain. any real
number Where it is used. the general ceiling; the embedded-correction claim

𝜁 (zeta) the direct depth-dependence of burden Whether going deeper generates correctable work by
itself, apart from the capability it produces. How it is measured. The direct exponent of recursive depth
in newly generated burden, holding capability fixed. Minus one in the registration’s model, which is what
it means for burden to follow the rate of capability gain rather than its level. Domain. any real number
Where it is used. the general ceiling

Where one letter means two things
A symbol that carries two meanings is the failure this page exists to prevent. Both readings are named;
neither is quietly dropped.

gamma: 3 distinct quantities
    • gamma the correction-leverage exponent, also called the correction-capacity exponent; the one
      in the ceiling law. Written chi from the working paper of 16 August 2026 onward, which is the
      name to use in any new writing; the registration still says gamma and the two are the same quantity
      (EQ-03)
    • gamma_N the residual-decay exponent, how fast leftover error falls with revision count at fixed
      capability (the depth studies; no equation in this register)
    • gamma_D the capability-to-depth conversion exponent, whose own finite-time ceiling is one divided
      by it (no equation in this register)
Why it matters. This is the collision with the worst history in the programme. The form alpha_crit =
1/gamma was retracted on 16 August 2026 for the correction-leverage reading, where it runs backwards.
The SAME form is correct algebra for the conversion reading, because a different dynamical system sits
underneath it. The two ceilings agree at exactly one half, which is the programme’s headline number, so a
wrong pairing survives casual review. Anyone writing gamma without saying which of the three they
mean can produce a sentence that is true under one reading, retracted under another, and unfalsifiable as
written. Say which one, every time. One further reading exists and it is a rename rather than a fourth
quantity: the ceiling-law reading is written chi from 16 August 2026, because gamma could no longer be
written safely. The chi entry says so; this entry now says it too, so the pointer runs both ways and a reader
meeting either symbol finds the other.
    Resolution. Write the full name in prose on first use in any document, and use a subscript only
alongside it. The bare letter gamma is reserved for the correction-leverage exponent of EQ-03 and means
nothing else.

beta: 5 distinct quantities
    • beta_C correction exponent, Law II, compared against k (EQ-04)
    • beta_L self-referential coupling, Foundational Theorem 2, giving alpha = 1/(1-beta_L) (EQ-05,
      EQ-06)
    • beta_H saturation steepness, a Hill coefficient (EQ-09)
    • beta_A alignment-scaling exponent (EQ-10)
    • beta_X boundary gap, a ratio of shortfalls (EQ-12)
Why it matters. A reader or a machine that conflates beta_C with beta_L gets the safety criterion exactly
backwards, because one is a correction rate compared against drift and the other is a coupling that feeds a
reciprocal. Paper X uses a bare beta for two different quantities within one paper.
    Resolution. This file subscripts all five. The corpus has not yet adopted the subscripts outside Paper
XIII, which introduced them independently and correctly. Propagation across the papers is outstanding,
and it belongs to the papers rather than to this register: this file records the convention, and each paper
adopts it at its next version.

What is still open
    • Is the exponent in the error-decay equation the same alpha as the capability-growth exponent, or a
      distinct alignment-specific one? Never stated.
    • The independence premise behind gamma = one half is undefended. It is the single assumption
      holding up the value two, and it is deliberately left open.
    • EQ-05 supplies an unexploited falsifier: measure beta_L independently, predict alpha, compare
      against the fitted alpha. No unit currently does this.
    • Cohen’s d and the programme’s dimensional d share a letter and cannot both be renamed, because
      one belongs to statistics rather than to this programme. Which of the programme’s own uses takes
      a subscript is an open decision.
    • Does build order actually change the corrector’s class enough to move the leverage exponent? The
      argument says order is a lever; whether pulling it separates the classes in practice is unmeasured,
      and it is the cheapest decisive study the programme has specified.
