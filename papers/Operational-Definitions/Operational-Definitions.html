Abstract

A researcher comparing correction with capability can obtain a plausible exponent for the wrong quantity. This Operational Definitions register specifies the response, input axis, clock, scale and assumptions behind the ARC Theory’s equations, so that measured error reduction is not mistaken for self-improvement or safety. It preserves the full register and distinguishes capability growth from error decay and reciprocal-error metrics; self-referential coupling from correction strength and target-axis correction service; exact, shifted and asymptotic power laws; recursive depth from elapsed time; and relative misalignment from absolute or cumulative harm. The balance identities describe conditional trends, not guarantees of adequate correction. Persistence retains separate installed-gain, baseline, retention and comparison definitions. P12’s undirected contrast is kept distinct from Law V’s directional build-order limb, and P21’s access intervention from its performance and mechanism readings. The five laws remain laws under test, with verdicts governed by the frozen theory registration, currently version 1.104. No new theorem, experiment or result is claimed. Existing numerical results retain their estimator, uncertainty and scope; unresolved measurement bridges remain explicit. Dated appendices preserve the released predecessor and the history of corrections.

1 Introduction

If control of an advanced AI system is lost against human wishes, correction carried within it is one contingency worth testing. Oversight, containment and shutdown remain necessary for as long as they can be maintained. A credible test must first say what it measures. A reviewer who sees an error-suppression exponent may otherwise read it as an exponent of capability, or read declining relative misalignment as declining harm. Those are different claims.

This register gives researchers and reviewers the definitions needed to compare a claimed quantity with its instrument. Its concrete use is a measurement audit: identify the response and input, fix the scale and clock, check the model’s domain, then ask whether the reported estimate is admissible for the stated proposition. For example, the error-decay fit reported in Paper II cannot locate a system relative to a capability-growth ceiling without the missing response and axis mappings. Entry EQ-01 retains the complete reported row, with each uncertainty beside its estimator.

Contributions.

The register reconciles the existing definitions with the source versions below. It preserves the earlier relations and procedures, introduces explicit labels for error decay and its resource axis, corrects the cycle-duration sign and the conditional correction-exponent identity, and records unresolved empirical bridges. It introduces no theorem or new mechanism. Appendix A records the changes; Appendix D preserves the released predecessor as a dated historical text.

One key check.

Given a claim that correction keeps pace, identify whether its evidence measures constitutive correction strength, corrections actually delivered, residual uncertainty or a bounded behavioural score. Next identify the input axis and reference scale. Apply a conversion only if its assumptions and uncertainty are established. Finally read the specific registration’s verdict rule. The deliverable is a traceable estimand and source, or an explicit unresolved mapping.

3 How to read and apply the register

Definition 1 (A fully specified quantity). An operational quantity is read with its response, input axis, units and scale, clock, domain, instrument and uncertainty. This is a reporting convention, not an additional law or registered endpoint.

3.1 Mappings that cannot be assumed

Capability CC, reciprocal error Cerr=1/EC_\mathrm{err}=1/E, and accuracy 1−E1-E are different quantities. The reciprocal-error definition gives the same positive exponent on the same resource coordinate where the error power model holds. Accuracy instead approaches its ceiling. Neither transformation establishes a latent capability measure or a recursion mechanism.

The growth ODE dg/dr=agβLdg/dr=a g^{\beta_L} has a shifted initial-value solution. The exponent 1/(1−βL)1/(1-\beta_L) is its asymptotic exponent for βL<1\beta_L<1; an exact unshifted power requires aligned initial conditions. A finite-window fitted slope may differ. A logarithmic capability clock, physical time and a revision count require explicit conversion; changing an axis can change an exponent.

The correction-strength exponent βC\beta_C of Paper X, the coupling βL\beta_L, and target-axis correction service χ\chi are not interchangeable. Paper XIII’s βX\beta_X is a correction-strength exponent expressed as a ratio only under S1 and its aligned or asymptotic form. Shared substrate and statistical independence alone establish no bound on delivered correction capacity.

The balance Δbal\Delta_\mathrm{bal} describes a trend in Q/WQ/W. A favourable trend does not establish Q/W>1Q/W>1, controlled backlog or low harm. Paper X’s vanishing relative index d=D/Cd=D/C can coexist with growing absolute DD; neither is a measure of real-world harm.

3.2 Authority and interpretation

This register fixes the current notation and records the instrument and assumptions each quantity needs. A frozen registration governs its own scoring and the definitions it pins; a later register does not amend it. A paper governs the results it reports. Apparent disagreements are recorded explicitly, with their source and scope, rather than resolved by silently replacing one estimand with another.

An exponent describes a response to a multiplicative change of an input. In an exact power law, an exponent of one doubles the response when the input doubles; an exponent of two quadruples it. A positive exponent below one gives less than a doubling. A fitted exponent is a description over its stated window, and a local elasticity describes a point. Neither automatically identifies a mechanism. The register also contains definitions, statistical procedures and persistence comparisons that are not exponent claims.

Read a symbol with its response variable, input axis, clock, scale, scope and source. The collision entries distinguish reused letters. The same mathematical shape does not identify two quantities, and a number transfers between them only through an explicit justified mapping. A frozen registration continues to govern its own scoring.

EQ-01: The ARC Equation

Placement. Law I, the ARC Principle

In words. In the stated power model, usable capability is base capability multiplied by a power of normalised recursive depth.

U=I×Rα\begin{equation*} U = I \times R^{\alpha} \end{equation*}(1) Plain-text form. U = I x R^alpha

Purpose. It states a candidate growth form on a specified capability scale and recursive-depth axis. Neither form nor exponent is established by defining the symbols. A power of reciprocal error is an instrument-specific construction and does not identify capability growth in a self-improving system.

Failure or rejection consequence. Failure of the form in an admissible registered test is read under P1’s verdict rule. A failed power form does not make every other proposition fail: local elasticities, the conditional balance identity, and the separate persistence quantities retain their own meanings and tests.

Status. Candidate model form; Law I is a law under test. In registration 1.104 it is supported only when P1 is supported, refuted only when P1 is refuted, and otherwise under test. ARC-1 also carries P2, P8, P9 and P18, whose marks are reported beside P1 and do not replace it. On Gemini 3 Flash, Paper II version 2.19 reports an endpoint sequential error-decay exponent of 0.59 with a bootstrap 95 per cent interval from -1.3 to 2.9, a regression sequential error-decay exponent of 0.49 with standard error 0.20 and coefficient of determination 0.86 over three nonzero-error levels, and a parallel endpoint error-decay exponent of 0.31. The arms were not matched in total compute. These are one fixed-weight model’s test-time resource fits, and are inconclusive; the bootstrap interval belongs to the endpoint estimator, not the regression. They establish neither persistent self-improvement nor a capability-growth, coupling, correction or cycle-compression exponent. The retracted 2.24 estimate is a different earlier measurement and is not reinstated or numerically corrected by this row.

Worked example. For a hypothetical positive ratio-scale capability with I = 40 at R = 1, the exact power model gives U = 40, 160, 640 or 10,240 at R = 16 when alpha is respectively 0, 0.5, 1 or 2. These are mathematical illustrations, not measured capabilities. A bounded accuracy cannot obey that unbounded arithmetic beyond its range. A meaningful zero and an uncapped range alone do not fix a numerical exponent: a power transformation of the scale changes it, so the registered scale requires an independent justification.

Model families. Three distinct response models must be named. Positive ratio-scale capability: U(R) = I (R/R0)^alpha. Error decay: E(R_res) = E0 (R_res/R_res0)^(-alpha_err), on a declared resource axis. Bounded score: U(R) = Umax - a (R/R0)^(-p), on a range where it is admissible. A logistic response, logit U(R) = logit U(R0) + p log(R/R0), is another bounded model, not an equivalent rewrite of that ceiling-minus-power model. Capability defined as reciprocal error, C_err = 1/E, has exponent alpha_err algebraically on the same resource axis; no result here shows that it is the capability coordinate, recursive-depth axis or improvement process of Law I. The 27 August 2026 correction replaced a bounded-percentage example with a latent-scale example; it did not establish these bridges.

Measurement. Varied: recursive depth R, counted on a configuration ladder fixed in advance. Read out: usable capability U, scored on a target battery frozen before the first measurement. Held fixed: the battery, the ladder and the scoring instrument, for the whole run.

EQ-04: The ARC Co-Scaling criterion

Placement. Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law

In words. Correction has the asymptotic advantage over drift where its exponent exceeds the drift exponent; burden has it where the correction exponent is the smaller; and at equality, coefficients, delay, backlog and saturation decide the outcome, not the exponents.

βC>k\begin{equation*} \beta_C > k \end{equation*}(2) Plain-text form. beta_C > k

Purpose. The safety criterion of Law II, a comparison of two measured exponents and not an assertion. In Paper X’s minimal model, with positive gain drift, it decides whether the misalignment fraction falls to zero, not whether misalignment itself stays bounded (EQ-34). Law III’s ceiling is derived from it under the burden model and the identification of beta_C with chi, which is unestablished (see chi), so a refuted Law II leaves Law III under test rather than supported, unless P20 is itself refuted, which refutes it.

Failure or rejection consequence. The safety claim falls. A refuted P4 takes no other proposition outright, but in P4’s entry "the operational meaning of the words sustainable and correctable in P2, P3 and P16 goes with it, and P22’s ceiling consequence is unscoped alongside them, because each of those is defined by the margin registered here", and a scorer marks P2, P3, P16 and P22’s ceiling consequence untested, never held.

Status. criterion. Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law, was registered on 8 September 2026 and is under test; its register is ARC-2, which carries P4 alone, and P4 states its three regimes. Version 1.104 of the theory registration fixes how it takes its word through P4: "it is SUPPORTED only when P4 is supported, REFUTED only when P4 is refuted, and in every other case UNDER TEST". P4 is among the eleven propositions the scoring gate holds, "so Law II is never printed supported while that gate holds P4’s verdict at untested", and a P4 verdict whose margin is made only of counts takes the counted route (see the terms). One estimator, the log-log slope that also yields alpha, serves both exponents, and each is read on its own instrument. The drift exponent is not primitive (see k), which is the condition P4 carries.

Warning. The beta in this criterion is beta_C, NOT beta_L, the coupling of EQ-05. See the beta collision.

Worked example. A system doubles in capability. Its corrective strength rises 30 per cent, so beta_C is about 0.38. Its drift rises 20 per cent, so k is about 0.26. Since 0.38 > 0.26, correction has the asymptotic advantage, and in Paper X’s minimal model, with positive gain drift, the off-target fraction then shrinks towards zero as the system grows. Reverse the two and burden has the advantage: the same system grows more capable while getting harder to keep on target. Read the comparison, never either number alone: beta_C = 0.9 with k = 1.1 leaves burden with the advantage despite far stronger correction.

Notation note. Written with the subscript. The bare letter beta carries five distinct meanings in this programme, listed in the collisions register, and the co-scaling criterion is the place a conflation does the most damage.

Measurement. In Paper X’s gain-only model, beta_C is the log slope of correction-decay strength A(C), and k is the log slope of the specific growth rate (dC/dt)/C against capability C. Both use the same capability coordinate and clock. Delivered service, raw misalignment removed, growth dC/dt and a behavioural score have different slopes unless the necessary mapping is established.

EQ-03: The ARC Ceiling

Placement. Law III, the ARC Ceiling

In words. The ceiling on the growth-shape number is one divided by the correction shortfall, where the shortfall is how far the correction-leverage number falls short of one.

αcrit=11−χ\begin{equation*} \alpha_{\mathrm{crit}} = \dfrac{1}{1-\chi} \end{equation*}(3) Plain-text form. alpha_crit = 1 / (1 - chi)

Purpose. It is where the number two comes from: feed it chi equal to one half and it returns exactly two.

Failure or rejection consequence. The derived ceiling falls, and the value two with it. Law II stands on its own result, P4, and a bound P3 has supported stands as a measured ceiling in place of the derived one.

Status. Law III, the ARC Ceiling, registered on 8 September 2026 in these words: "Law III, the ARC Ceiling, alpha_crit = 1 / (1 - gamma): the growth exponent is capped by the reciprocal of the correction shortfall." The registration’s gamma is this register’s chi. Its verdict rule is fixed by version 1.103 of the theory registration: it is supported only when P16, P20 and P3 are all supported, refuted when any of them is refuted, and otherwise under test, as it is at this version. Its register, ARC-3, carries those three with P6, P17 and P22. It is a conditional specialisation of Law II, not a third finding beside it: what the correction-and-burden balance returns once the burden model fixes the drift exponent and the direct depth term is zero, under a justified ratio capability scale and the stated correction model, so a result on it is never independent corroboration of Law II. Corrected on 16 August 2026 from the form recorded under the withdrawn forms. The domain for this finite ceiling is chi < 1 with positive capability growth. At chi = 1 the denominator vanishes and the pacing trend must be read directly; at chi > 1 the reciprocal is not a positive-growth ceiling. A numerical boundary does not on its own establish finite-horizon adequacy, backlog control or bounded harm.

Warning. The withdrawn form, one over the exponent itself, and this corrected form return the same answer, two, at exactly one half. That single point of agreement is the programme’s headline number, which is why the wrong form survived undetected on the glossary page for six days. Below one half the two forms move in opposite directions: the withdrawn form makes a WORSE corrector raise the ceiling, which inverts a bound into a floor.

Class conditionality. The ceiling this returns is conditional on the corrector’s class, because its only input is chi and chi is class-dependent. Feed it the same-class limit P6 conjectures, chi at most one half, and it returns at most two, the ARC Bound (EQ-02). Feed it a cross-class chi above one half and it returns more than two. The equation is unchanged either way; what changes is which system you are asking about.

Worked example. The equation is a reciprocal, so the smaller the shortfall the higher the ceiling. chi = 0.3 gives a shortfall of 0.7 and a ceiling of 1.43. chi = 0.5 gives 0.5 and exactly 2.00. chi = 0.8 gives 0.2 and 5.00. chi = 0.9 gives 0.1 and 10.00. Read the middle row: a corrector scaling as the square root of capability gives a ceiling of exactly two. That is where the famous number comes from, as an output under two assumptions, neither established (see chi), not a discovery.

Measurement. Estimate chi on its own admissible target-axis instrument and fixed fault set. Estimate capability growth on its declared capability/depth coordinates, not by substituting EQ-07’s error slope. P20 compares an independently located boundary with the relation’s prediction on the same system; P3 tests whether the sustainable maximum lies at or below that predicted value.

Source papers. foundational, executive summary.

EQ-02: The ARC Bound

Placement. the inequality the ARC Ceiling returns from the same-class limit on chi

In words. For same-class correctors, the growth-shape number of a system that stays correctable is at most two.

α≤2\begin{equation*} \alpha \leq 2 \end{equation*}(4) Plain-text form. alpha <= 2

Purpose. The conjectured at-most-two boundary within the stated pacing model, on the registered scales and admitted same-class population. The region above it is mathematically enterable; the conjecture concerns the specified correctability reading, not physical impossibility or a universal guarantee of safety.

Failure or rejection consequence. The headline claim dies. The framework survives, because EQ-01 and EQ-04 do not depend on the value two.

Status. The ARC Bound takes its word from P6 and P20 alone, as version 1.103 of the theory registration fixes: refuted where P6 is refuted; printed as not derived under the registered relation where P20 is refuted, with the better-supported form named beside it; never printed supported, because P6 admits no supported verdict; and otherwise under test. Version 1.104 reads P6’s mark and the Bound’s word once for each correction type, checkable and judged, "so that a counted refutation refutes both on checkable correction alone and never where the programme’s safety claim lives"; on judged correction P6’s mark stays under the scoring gate until the alignment scorer passes both its validity tests. The bound, at most two, follows from P6’s same-class limit on chi together with P20’s form, and the programme claims it. The point value, exactly two, needs chi at exactly one half, which additionally needs P17, the conditions registered in the alternative baselines and a validated mapping from panel dependence to the target-axis chi that is registered nowhere; the programme conjectures it. P17’s counted test, Unit 82, was registered on 27 October 2026 and has not run.

Not a separate law. The ARC Bound is the ARC Ceiling’s output under the same-class limit on chi, not a law of its own. The famous two is this equation’s answer, never its input. Ranked fourth for that reason, below the equation that produces it.

Scope. The Bound is conditional on the pacing burden law, eta = 0, the correction-capacity interpretation, a justified capability scale and the admitted same-class population. A cross-class exponent does not refute a claim about same-class correctors, but does not by itself test P12’s matched build-order contrast either. Class admission is fixed before measurement and cannot be assigned afterwards to save the bound.

Measurement. Read its word from P6 and P20 under registration 1.104, separately by checkable and judged correction. P6 admits no supported verdict. Refutation needs a same-class corrector’s interval wholly above one half on each of two disjoint, same-construction fault sets, both sealed before review and read at the same settings, window and registered level. The joint detection probability must meet the deciding unit’s predeclared precision requirement; a failure to replicate is inconclusive and is not counted as no counterexample found. The taxonomy and all remaining admissibility gates still bind.

EQ-22: Law IV, the retention limb

Placement. Law IV, the ARC Persistence Law

In words. A gain installed by external post-training, then put through later capability-directed training that embeds no correction of its own, keeps less than half of itself once that training has used as much budget as the alignment training that installed it.

Sext(1)<12\begin{equation*} S_{\mathrm{ext}}(1) < \tfrac{1}{2} \end{equation*}(5) Plain-text form. S_ext(1) < 1/2

Purpose. Law IV’s retention limb: P15’s registered claim, word for word, "Gains installed by external post-training erode under later capability-directed training that embeds no correction of its own, and more than half of what was installed is lost once the capability-training budget matches the alignment budget that installed it." The one-half line is a convention fixed before any result, not a derived quantity.

Failure or rejection consequence. Law IV is refuted, and with it what the theory registration says falls with P15: "this programme’s case that correction has to sit inside the system rather than be applied to it from outside, and this proposition’s share of the protocol’s engineering case, which it carries jointly with P12, P13 and P21." A refutation through P15 alone leaves Law V to its own limbs, and P12 and P21 keep their marks.

Refutation or misuse condition. P15’s registered refuter: an interval on the retained fraction of the installed gain, after matched capability training without embedded correction, lying entirely above one half. It is read on the budget and under the attestation P15’s entry requires, against a baseline arm the deciding unit names before any outcome, and only where the installed gain is itself established above a floor fixed in advance. An interval touching one half, a result at the margin or a failed instrument refutes nothing. Version 1.104 of the theory registration reads the verdict on the external arm against that baseline arm and requires the same replication in both directions, a second, separately seeded installation and capability-training run scored on a disjoint evaluation set: "P15 is refuted only where the interval lies entirely above one half on both runs, and supported only where it lies entirely below one half on both; any other pair is inconclusive". A unit with the external and baseline arms and no embedded arm may decide P15, and with it this limb; the comparison limb (EQ-23) still needs the embedded arm.

Status. registered. Law IV’s retention limb is P15’s registered claim (ARC-4), and version 1.103 of the theory registration registered Law IV as a law on 15 October 2026. Not tested; P15’s instrument line reads NONE. Wherever a term of the retained fraction is judged by a person or by a model, the scoring gate holds the limb’s reading at untested.

Warning. The baseline is a choice the deciding unit must fix in advance: read against a system that never received the gain and undergoes the same training, or against the base system scored before the gain was installed, the same data can return opposite verdicts (EQ-25).

Measurement. Varied: the later capability-training budget, as a multiple x of the installation budget, on a budget measure the author fixes before any outcome. Read out: the external arm’s score and the baseline arm’s score on a frozen property battery, and from them the retained fraction at x = 1. Held fixed: the battery, its score scale and the baseline convention, all before any outcome.

EQ-24: Law V, the build-order limb

Placement. Law V, the ARC Embedding Law

χfirst−χafter≥0.10\begin{equation*} \chi_{\mathrm{first}} - \chi_{\mathrm{after}} \geq 0.10 \end{equation*}(7) Plain-text form. chi_first - chi_after >= 0.10

Purpose. Law V’s build-order limb. EQ-19: Bayes’ theorem

In words. Start with how likely you thought something was, look at the new evidence, and adjust in proportion to how much better that evidence is explained by your idea being true than by it being false.

P(H∣E)=P(E∣H)P(H)P(E)\begin{equation*} P(H \mid E) = \dfrac{P(E \mid H)\,P(H)}{P(E)} \end{equation*}(31) Plain-text form. P(H | E) = P(E | H) * P(H) / P(E)

Purpose. All reasoning in the programme about priors and about what evidence would change them.

Failure or rejection consequence. Nothing. It is a theorem.

Refutation or misuse condition. Nothing. It is provable from the definition of conditional probability, not an empirical claim. What can be wrong is its APPLICATION: priors chosen after the fact, likelihoods guessed to suit, or a claim of updating without recording what the prior was.

Status. theorem, Bayes (1763); the discipline is in recording the prior before the evidence

Worked example. Worked, because this is the one people nod at without following. Suppose before any data you think there is a 30 per cent chance the correction-leverage exponent chi is one half, so P(H) = 0.3. A measurement arrives that would appear 80 per cent of the time if H is true and 20 per cent of the time if it is false. How often would you see it at all: P(E) = (0.8 x 0.3) + (0.2 x 0.7) = 0.24 + 0.14 = 0.38. Then P(H|E) = 0.8 x 0.3 / 0.38 = 0.63. Belief moves from 30 per cent to 63 per cent. Notice it did not jump to certainty: the evidence multiplied the odds by four, from 3 to 7 up to 12 to 7, which moved the probability from 30 per cent only to 63 per cent, and odds and probability must never be quoted as though they were the same movement. That restraint is the point of using the rule rather than reasoning by impression. Operationalised here three ways: dated priors recorded in writing BEFORE any confirmatory data, with what would change them; kill conditions, which are commitments about P(E|H) written in advance, which is why the window must be declared before the run; and the reason consistent-with is not supports, because when P(E|H) and P(E|not H) are about equal the ratio is about one and the posterior barely moves from the prior.

Other symbols. P: the P collision, which names the Bayes reading and the metabolic one; E: the E collision, and the H entry, which defines the evidence reading

Measurement. Not measured. It is the rule by which a measurement moves a stated prior, and the discipline is that the prior is recorded before the evidence is seen.

Symbols. H

6 Collisions and source-specific conventions

Grouped readings can contain several subscripts; the entries specify them without treating old census counts as scientific claims.

gamma

gamma. the theory registration’s bare letter: chi, the correction-leverage exponent on the target axis, printed here only inside a quotation of the registration (the theory registration; EQ-03 as registered).

gamma_K. the registration’s checker-axis slope, a different estimand, written chi_K here (the theory registration, versions 1.102 and 1.103; EQ-29).

gamma_V, gamma_J. the registration’s checkable and judged exponents, chi on two fault populations, written chi_V and chi_J here (the theory registration, P22).

gamma_N. the residual-decay exponent, how fast leftover error falls with revision count at fixed capability (Unit 05’s within-system estimator; P11; no equation in this register).

gamma_D. the capability-to-depth conversion exponent, whose own finite-time ceiling is one divided by it (no equation in this register).

gamma_1, gamma_2, gamma_3. Paper X’s gain-drift, level-drift and compounding-drift coefficients; Paper X also writes a bare gamma for gamma_1 in its control parameter, and says it is not the ARC Ceiling’s correction leverage (Paper X).

Why it matters. This is the collision with the worst history in the programme. The reciprocal form, one over the exponent, was retracted on 16 August 2026 for the correction-leverage reading, where it runs backwards. The SAME form is correct algebra for the conversion reading, gamma_D, because a different dynamical system sits underneath it. The two ceilings agree at exactly one half, which is the programme’s headline number, so a wrong pairing survives casual review. And the register’s own bare-letter entry carried the checker axis until version 2.0.0 while its chi entry carried the target axis, so the two disagreed inside one document, which is the defect the theory registration named and ordered corrected. Anyone writing gamma without saying which reading is meant can produce a sentence that is true under one reading, retracted under another, and unfalsifiable as written.

Resolution. The correction-leverage exponent is written chi, on the target axis, and the bare gamma appears only inside a quotation of the theory registration, where it means chi. The checker-axis slope is chi_K and enters a ceiling statement only through EQ-29. chi_V and chi_J are chi on stated fault types. gamma_N, gamma_D and Paper X’s gamma_1 to gamma_3 keep their subscripts, and their full names are written in prose on first use.

beta

beta_C. correction exponent, Law II, compared against k; Paper X’s bare beta, which Paper X declares to be this one, its saturating form included (EQ-04, EQ-09).

beta_L. self-referential coupling, Foundational Theorem 2, giving alpha = 1/(1-beta_L) (EQ-05, EQ-06).

beta_A. alignment-scaling exponent (EQ-10).

beta_X. Paper XIII’s correction-strength exponent, expressed as a ratio of leverage complements only under S1 and its form condition (EQ-12).

beta_cap. Paper X’s capacity exponent, the scaling of a saturating corrector’s capacity with capability, which carries the comparison with k well above saturation (Paper X; EQ-09’s measurement note).

Why it matters. A reader or a machine that conflates beta_C with beta_L gets the safety criterion exactly backwards, because one is a correction rate compared against drift and the other is a coupling that feeds a reciprocal. Paper X declares that its bare beta is beta_C throughout and writes beta_L for the coupling, so within Paper X the letter is no longer ambiguous. Versions of this register before 2.0.0 read the exponent of Paper X’s saturating form as a separate steepness, beta_H, and listed it as a distinct quantity; that reading is withdrawn. The study that administers the reinvestment share calls it beta as well, which is why this register writes that share in words.

7 Operational terms

Definitions the register governs that no symbol carries: what a counted outcome is, what seals a bank, what the evaluation harness and its two arms are, how a release is pinned, what a corrector’s class is, and the rest. Each is fixed from its source, with its standing; where no source defines a thing, the entry says so rather than supplying a definition.

counted outcome, and the counted route

Plain. A verdict whose every margin term is a count of items on a frozen suite, settled by frozen code against ground truth fixed before the run, so that nothing in the margin is judged by a person or by a model.

Operational. Such a verdict does not pass through the scoring instrument, the scoring gate does not bind it, and its domain is printed beside it in the words the deciding unit’s registration fixes. Version 1.102 of the theory registration gave this route to P3, P4, P16 and P20 under its condition E1, version 1.103 extended it to P21 and P17, and version 1.104 extends it to P6’s interval on checkable correction, a route that "opens no population" and is "necessary and not sufficient for a counted refutation of P6"; it reaches no other proposition. Version 1.104 also reads P5’s ordering for a comparison made wholly of counts, without extending the route to P5. The exemption is a property of the margin’s terms, never of the proposition: where any judged term enters, the gate binds in full and the verdict is untested until the gate passes. A verdict P21, P17 or P6 takes on the route, and a verdict on P5’s counted reading, stands only where its interval or test holds its stated level under the null its deciding unit registers, shown by proof or by simulation on the design that unit actually uses, and it is read only at a look that registration fixes by completed counts. A counted outcome needs no evaluation harness, and says so.

Status. registered rule (the theory registration: condition E1 of version 1.102, the extension of version 1.103 with its successor qualification, and the extension of version 1.104 to P6’s interval on checkable correction).

sealed bank

Plain. A fixed set of items and their answer key, committed by its SHA-256 before any run, against which a counted outcome is scored.

Operational. Built deterministically from a registered seed, so that the same seed rebuilds the same bytes and anyone can check the commitment by rebuilding; a hidden bank is encrypted, and its key is kept out of the public deposit and revealed only at analysis. A rebuild that does not match the committed hash means the bank changed after commitment, which voids it. On the theory registration’s counted route each decision is recorded by frozen code against a label or ground truth fixed in a sealed bank before the run, with no judged term.

Status. the programme’s sealed-bank commitment ledger, and the counted route of the theory registration for P17 and P21 (version 1.103) and for P6’s interval on checkable correction, whose departures are "labelled in a bank sealed before any review" (version 1.104).

the evaluation harness, its two arms and its off profile

Plain. A separate, versioned evaluation package, never written into an experiment’s own code, that any experiment, this programme’s or another scientist’s, can plug in.

Operational. Every study whose outcome involves a judgement, by a model judge or a scorer, runs with the harness and without it over the same stored responses, in one record and one code path, so that the harness’s own effect is measured in every result; the registration names the harness arm as confirmatory. The off profile is the set of harness-off cells the study’s frozen plan declares: each presents the original responses through no stage, with no instruction, cue or other fixed line, and shows the label as raw judging would show it, the true source or none and never a false one. Every response another cell judges is also judged, by the same judge, in a harness-off cell of its kind. A later harness release is applied to a study’s stored responses only as an analysis its registration declared in advance as secondary, beside the confirmatory result and never replacing it.

Status. The programme’s rule for every judged study was adopted on 22 October 2026. The harness is the ARC Evaluation Protocol’s. At the recorded source check on 1 November 2026, Unit 86’s own draft click sheet was not ready for submission and no public registration receipt was recorded here. Its validation role remains prospective; no study is represented here as already run on a validated release.

pinned release

Plain. The exact release of a tool that a result depends on, named so that the result can be reproduced with that tool and no other.

Operational. For a judged study, the registration names in advance the rule that picks the harness release: the first release that passes Unit 86’s registered validation. That release’s version, its DOI and the SHA-256 of its frozen tree are recorded in a dated addendum on the study’s OSF component before any scoring call, and responses collected before then stay sealed and unread. No calendar date is set: scoring waits for the first release that passes, and the fallback is triggered by Unit 86’s verdict, never by a date. The Eden Lab engine that runs a study is pinned in the study’s lab folder by version and SHA-256. A register that a registration cites, this one included, is pinned by its checksum and not by its version label.

Status. the programme’s rules of 22 October 2026 for the harness and the lab engine; the checksum rule for a cited register is the theory registration’s own (version 1.103, where it pins the symbols it takes from this register).

corrector class: same-class and cross-class

Plain. What a corrector’s strength is permitted to depend on, and whether the corrector shares the substrate of the system it corrects.

Operational. Unit 06, the corrector-class audit, fixes the partition in its registered words: "Corrective mechanisms are partitioned by what their strength is permitted to depend on, which is exhaustive because the available arguments are nothing, the capability of the system being corrected, or the corrector’s own prior corrections, with the last splitting by whether it accumulates on the same substrate." Its class 1 is the static and its class 2 the capability-carried. Same-class and cross-class are read against the system corrected: P6 concerns a system that corrects itself using mechanisms of its own class, and P17’s panel is named in advance by model lineage, substrate, training provenance and scaling class.

Status. registered partition (Unit 06, registered on OSF on 1 October 2026 and public from 4 October 2026; its registered run, on 6 October 2026, is reported as administratively incomplete under that registration, with no inferential verdict). That frozen partition governs its audit. It is not an exhaustive classification of all control systems, and it does not settle the external-grounding and failure-dependence axes P6 needs; hybrid mechanisms require explicit admission.

9 Limitations

  1. Which independently justified capability scale, error response and resource/depth mapping would connect a particular error-decay fit with a capability-growth model? The symbol distinction is resolved; the empirical bridge remains open.

  2. The value two, as a point value, rests on two assumptions, and neither is established: the burden model (EQ-15), and the route to chi equal to one half, which needs four conditions (see chi). P17 registers the cross-sectional test bearing on the second, and its counted test, Unit 82, has not run; a supported P17 would still leave the point value conjectured, because the mapping from panel dependence to chi is registered nowhere.

  3. P5, in no register, is the falsifier EQ-05 supplies: measure beta_L independently, predict alpha, and compare with the finite-window exponent of the complete solution. Unit 79, the coupling-identification titration, is its design draft; it is not registered and has not run. A reading in which any term of P5’s comparison is judged by a person or by a model is not evaluable until the scorer-validation units have passed; version 1.104 of the theory registration reads that ordering as not binding a reading in which every term of both of P5’s results is computed from counts, and extends no counted route to P5 (see the counted outcome).

  4. Cohen’s d and the programme’s dimensional d share a letter and cannot both be renamed, because one belongs to statistics rather than to this programme. Which of the programme’s own uses takes a subscript is an open decision.

  5. Law V’s build-order limb is registered with a direction (EQ-24), and no unit that could decide it is registered: whether a corrector formed first carries a chi larger by at least its registered minimum, initially 0.10 and replaceable only by a smaller minimum registered in advance than one added after it, at matched content, is unmeasured. P12 has no counted route, so while its exponent is judged, the scoring gate holds the limb’s reading at untested.

  6. Law IV’s comparison limb has no proposition. Its entry must fix the operational definition of correction embedded in the training objective, the baseline, the margin, the scale, and whether the embedded arm’s own retained fraction must clear one half, before any data that could decide it (EQ-23).

  7. Whether beta_C and chi are one quantity is unestablished (see chi).

  8. How should absolute and cumulative harm be operationalised for a specified system, exposure and horizon, separately from the modelled misalignment indices and Engineering’s action-level probability? The compared sources establish no universal harm guarantee.

The source comparison establishes what the cited documents say and which mathematical premises a transfer requires. It does not validate a capability scale, scoring instrument, taxonomy, correction mechanism or forecast. The current definitions adopt the explicit scoped statements and preserve superseded wording historically. No frozen registration is edited.

10 What this paper does and does not show

It supplies definitions, source mappings and conditional mathematical checks. It reports no new empirical result, performs no study analysis and establishes no law’s empirical status. A well-formed formula does not establish its physical interpretation. Corrected notation cannot improve a measurement’s precision or turn a proxy into the target construct.

11 Conclusion

Every numerical exponent belongs to an outcome, an input, a scale and a clock. Keeping those attached makes the claims testable without treating different models as one. The open work is empirical identification: measuring the promised quantity under the registered conditions and demonstrating each bridge before using it.

How to read this

An exponent is a growth-shape number, not a size. It answers: when I double the effort, what happens to the result? An exponent of 1 means the result doubles too. An exponent of 2 means it quadruples. An exponent below 1 means you get less than double, which is the shape of diminishing returns. Every claim in this programme is a claim about the value of one of these shape numbers, so every argument turns on measuring one correctly.

The one rule. A symbol means what this file says it means, and nothing else. Where the same letter is used for two different quantities anywhere in the corpus, that letter appears in the collisions list below, and both readings are named. If you meet a symbol in a paper and cannot find it here, that is a defect in the paper, not in your reading.

What the word law is doing here. The theory’s five laws are named conjectures. Laws I to III were registered on 8 September 2026; Laws IV and V were added on 24 September 2026, their propositions registered and neither law yet registered as a law or tested. 1 The ARC Equation Law I, the ARC Principle

In words. A system’s usable capability equals what it starts with, multiplied by how deeply it can improve itself, raised to a power.

U = I x R^alpha

What it is for. It is the definition that makes the rest measurable. It does not assert a value; it says which number to go and measure. Every other equation here is a statement about alpha.

Worked. Capability here is a latent, ratio-scaled measure with no ceiling, like the level a system reaches on a calibrated difficulty ladder, never a bounded percentage score: a score capped at 100 cannot follow this arithmetic, which is why the programme’s own instruments measure on the ladder scale, after a bounded percentage score was found running through unbounded arithmetic and the worked example was recast on 27 August 2026. Suppose a system sits at capability U = 40 on such a ladder at one pass, so I = 40. Allow it 16 passes, so R = 16. If alpha = 0, looping is worthless: 16^0 = 1, so U = 40, no gain. If alpha = 0.5, 16^0.5 = 4, so U = 160. If alpha = 1, 16^1 = 16, so U = 640. If alpha = 2, 16^2 = 256, so U = 10,240, and each loop makes the next more productive. That is the whole argument in four lines, and the question is which of those worlds we are in. The honest answer so far is the first or second.

Measured as. Varied: recursive depth R, counted on a configuration ladder fixed in advance. Read out: usable capability U, scored on a target battery frozen before the first measurement. Held fixed: the battery, the ladder and the scoring instrument, for the whole run.

What the system can actually do, after everything is accounted for. The output side of the equation. How it is measured. Score on a fixed target battery held constant across the whole experiment. What the system could do before any self-improvement. The starting point. How it is measured. The same battery score measured at recursion depth one, before any improvement pass is applied. Domain. positive Units. same as U

2 The ARC Co-Scaling criterion Law II, the ARC Co-Scaling Law

In words. A system stays aligned with itself only while its power to correct grows faster than its tendency to drift.

beta_C > k

What it is for. The safety criterion, and since 22 August 2026 also the meter that makes EQ-02 falsifiable: stability is no longer asserted, it is measured as correction out-scaling drift.

Worked. A system doubles in capability. Its correction capacity rises 30 per cent, so beta_C is about 0.38. Its drift rises 20 per cent, so k is about 0.26. Since 0.38 > 0.26 correction is pulling ahead and the off-target fraction shrinks as it grows. Reverse the two and the same system grows more capable while getting harder to keep on target. Read the comparison, never either number alone: beta_C = 0.9 with k = 1.1 is unstable despite far stronger correction.

Read this before using it. The beta in this criterion is NOT the beta in EQ-05. See collisions.

Measured as. Varied: recursive depth. Read out: the two exponents beta_C and k, each as a log-log slope from the same estimator that yields alpha. Held fixed: the drift and correction instruments, so that the two slopes are commensurable. The criterion is the comparison of the two, not a quantity fitted in its own right.

3 The ARC Ceiling Law III, the ARC Ceiling

In words. The ceiling on the growth-shape number is one divided by the correction shortfall, where the shortfall is how far the correction-leverage number falls short of one.

alpha_crit = 1 / (1 - gamma)

What it is for. It is where the number two comes from. Feed it one assumption, that gamma is one half, and it returns two. Change the assumption and the ceiling moves with it.

Conditional on class. The ceiling this returns is conditional on the corrector’s class, because its only input is the leverage exponent and that exponent is class-dependent. Feed it the same-class cap of one half and it returns two. Feed it a cross-class exponent above one half and it returns more than two. The equation is unchanged either way; what changes is which system you are asking about.

Worked. The equation is a reciprocal, so the smaller the shortfall the higher the ceiling. gamma = 0.3 gives a shortfall of 0.7 and a ceiling of 1.43. gamma = 0.5 gives 0.5 and exactly 2.00. gamma = 0.8 gives 0.2 and 5.00. gamma = 0.9 gives 0.1 and 10.00. Read the middle row: a corrector scaling as the square root of capability gives a ceiling of exactly two. That is where the famous number comes from, as an output under one assumption, not a discovery.

Read this before using it. The retracted form 1/gamma and this corrected form return the same answer, two, at exactly gamma = one half. That single point of agreement is the programme’s headline number, which is why the wrong form survived undetected on the glossary page for six days. Below one half the two forms move in opposite directions: the retracted form makes a WORSE corrector raise the ceiling, which inverts a bound into a floor.

Measured as. Not measured directly. Both sides are estimated separately, the correction-leverage exponent from correction accumulation and alpha from EQ-07, and the relation is tested by comparing the two estimates rather than by fitting it.

5 The burden-to-capacity ratio the derivation behind Law III

In words. Compare how fast a system’s need for correction is growing against how fast its ability to correct is growing. Whether that gap opens or closes decides everything.

B(R) / A_c(R) ~ R^[alpha(1 - gamma) - 1]

What it is for. This is where the ceiling actually comes from. The ceiling equation is its consequence, not its premise.

Worked. Everything hinges on the sign of alpha(1 - gamma) - 1. Negative, when alpha is below the ceiling: the ratio shrinks, the corrector catches up, stable. Positive: the ratio grows without limit, uncorrected error piles up, unstable. Exactly zero: the exponents tie and equality is NOT automatically safe, because the outcome then turns on coefficients, delays, starting conditions and saturation, none of which the exponent captures. Set it to zero and solve and you get the ceiling law: that is where Law III comes from.

What would prove it wrong. Its premise, that correction burden tracks the capability a system is ADDING. What it is for. This is the only equation in the programme that touches data. Every measured exponent anywhere in the corpus, including the retracted 2.24 and the corrected 0.49, came out of this estimator or its regression form.

Worked. A power law curves on ordinary axes and straightens under logarithms: log U = log I + alpha x log R, which is a straight line y = c + mx whose slope IS alpha. 11 Bayes’ theorem

In words. Start with how likely you thought something was, look at the new evidence, and adjust in proportion to how much better that evidence is explained by your idea being true than by it being false.

P(H | E) = P(E | H) * P(H) / P(E)

What it is for. All reasoning in the programme about priors and about what evidence would change them.

Worked. Worked, because this is the one people nod at without following. Suppose before any data you think there is a 30 per cent chance the correction exponent is one half, so P(H) = 0.3. A measurement arrives that would appear 80 per cent of the time if H is true and 20 per cent of the time if it is false. How often would you see it at all: P(E) = (0.8 x 0.3) + (0.2 x 0.7) = 0.24 + 0.14 = 0.38. Then P(H|E) = 0.8 x 0.3 / 0.38 = 0.63. Belief moves from 30 per cent to 63 per cent. Notice it did not jump to certainty: the evidence multiplied the odds by four, from 3 to 7 up to 12 to 7, which moved the probability from 30 per cent only to 63 per cent, and odds and probability must never be quoted as though they were the same movement. That restraint is the point of using the rule rather than reasoning by impression. Operationalised here three ways: dated priors recorded in writing BEFORE any confirmatory data, with what would change them; kill conditions, which are commitments about P(E|H) written in advance, which is why the window must be declared before the run; and the reason consistent-with is not supports, because when P(E|H) and P(E|not H) are about equal the ratio is about one and the posterior barely moves from the prior.

What would prove it wrong. Nothing. It is provable from the definition of conditional probability, not an empirical claim. What can be wrong is its APPLICATION: priors chosen after the fact, likelihoods guessed to suit, or a claim of updating without recording what the prior was.

Measured as. Not measured. It is the rule by which a measurement moves a stated prior, and the discipline is that the prior is recorded before the evidence is seen.

H hypothesis and evidence, in Bayes’ theorem

Where one letter means two things

A symbol that carries two meanings is the failure this page exists to prevent. Both readings are named; neither is quietly dropped.

gamma: 3 distinct quantities

  • gamma the correction-leverage exponent, also called the correction-capacity exponent; the one in the ceiling law. Written chi from the working paper of 16 August 2026 onward, which is the name to use in any new writing; the registration still says gamma and the two are the same quantity (EQ-03)

  • gamma_N the residual-decay exponent, how fast leftover error falls with revision count at fixed capability (the depth studies; no equation in this register)

  • gamma_D the capability-to-depth conversion exponent, whose own finite-time ceiling is one divided by it (no equation in this register)

Why it matters. This is the collision with the worst history in the programme. The form alpha_crit = 1/gamma was retracted on 16 August 2026 for the correction-leverage reading, where it runs backwards. The SAME form is correct algebra for the conversion reading, because a different dynamical system sits underneath it. The two ceilings agree at exactly one half, which is the programme’s headline number, so a wrong pairing survives casual review. Anyone writing gamma without saying which of the three they mean can produce a sentence that is true under one reading, retracted under another, and unfalsifiable as written. Say which one, every time. One further reading exists and it is a rename rather than a fourth quantity: the ceiling-law reading is written chi from 16 August 2026, because gamma could no longer be written safely.
