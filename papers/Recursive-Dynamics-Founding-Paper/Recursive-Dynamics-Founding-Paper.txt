Abstract
--------
Machines that improve themselves using their own outputs now exist and work, and
there is no accepted science of what limits them. This paper founds that
science, Recursive Dynamics, and stakes its claim the way thermodynamics was
staked in 1824: by computing a bound that any such machine must obey if the
claim is right, publishing the instruments that measure its terms, and
preregistering by timestamp the experiments whose nulls favour the rival view.
The field takes the self-improvement loop as its native object and names five
state variables, conjectured to be substrate-independent: capability, recursive
depth, correction rate, drift and correction leverage. It carries five laws as
named conjectures with statuses declared. One, the ARC Ceiling, is derived in
full from two lines of assumption; two, added on 24 September 2026, have their
mathematics set out at version 1.0 and are neither registered as laws nor
tested. The field locates its identity, as thermodynamics does, in its
impossibilities: if the laws hold, self-improvement cannot outrun its own
correction, the growth exponent is capped by the reciprocal of the correction
shortfall, and no ladder of same-class oversight climbs past its ceiling by
adding rungs. The paper states what would make the field wrong, tries to house
its questions inside twelve neighbouring fields, gives each of fifty-one
objections a disposition, and prints the four results that would dissolve the
field. Its honest status is the Carnot stage: one memoir, instruments built,
decisive measurements preregistered by timestamp and not run, staked where
failure will be as visible as success.

Key findings (quotable)
-----------------------
- Machines that improve themselves using their own outputs now exist and work, and there is no accepted science of what limits them; the paper proposes that science, Recursive Dynamics, taking the self-improvement loop as its native object with five substrate-independent state variables: capability, recursive depth, correction rate, drift and correction leverage.
- Three laws are carried as named conjectures with statuses declared, of which one, the ARC Ceiling (alpha_crit = 1/(1 - gamma)), is derived in full from two lines of assumption; the ARC Bound (alpha at or below two) is the Ceiling's value at gamma = 1/2, and the correction leverage gamma has never been measured, by anyone, which the paper names as its single most consequential open measurement.
- The claim is stated as prohibitions so that concessions cannot be mistaken for silence: the field forbids a stable system holding its growth exponent above the ceiling for a sustained run, a same-class corrector whose correction leverage measures wholly above one half, a corrector-class ratio pinned at 1.00 with tight intervals where the cap should bind, and monotone returns with no interior maximum as reinvestment rises; any of these, observed under the registered analyses, makes the field wrong.
- Before claiming a field, the paper does what a demarcation usually refuses: it tries to house its questions inside twelve neighbouring fields in their own variables, concedes what each already holds, prints where every part would go if the field died, and isolates the one coupling no host can state: a cap on correction leverage that depends on what the corrector is made of, coupled to a growth exponent in recursive depth.
- Fifty-two objections in nine groups are printed in their strongest form with dispositions: thirty-nine conceded in full or in part, eleven rejected with reasons, two handed to experiment or to reading, including the author's own objection that the field is merely the ARC Theory renamed, rejected with a separability test the dissolution map can run.
- The name carries its own kill condition: it dies with its object, not with any one law's stated form; a reader who rejects all five laws and keeps the measurement is still inside the field, by the minimal commitment; and the paper's honest status is the Carnot stage: one memoir, instruments built, decisive measurements registered as drafts and not yet run, no replication by anyone outside the programme.
