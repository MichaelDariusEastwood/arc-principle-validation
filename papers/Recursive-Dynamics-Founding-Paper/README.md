## Summary

From the abstract (version 3.5):

> Machines that improve themselves using their own outputs now exist and work, and there is no accepted science of what limits them. This paper founds that science, Recursive Dynamics, and stakes its claim the way thermodynamics was staked in 1824: by computing a bound that any such machine must obey if the claim is right, publishing the instruments that measure its terms, and preregistering by timestamp the experiments whose nulls favour the rival view. ... It carries five laws as named conjectures under test, with statuses declared. One, the ARC Ceiling, is derived in full from two lines of assumption; two, added on 24 September 2026 and registered as laws on 15 October 2026 in version 1.103 of the theory registration, are not yet tested, and their mathematics, set out at version 1.1, is in development. ... Its honest status is the Carnot stage: one memoir, instruments built, decisive measurements preregistered by timestamp and not run, staked where failure will be as visible as success.
